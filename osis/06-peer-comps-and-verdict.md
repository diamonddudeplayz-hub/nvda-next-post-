## The verdict — and the honest catch

**This clears your bar and I'd act on it — but not as a full position, and here's the straight
reason:** the BIG catalyst (CBP/Big-Beautiful-Bill revenue + the margin inflection) is **mostly FY28**.
Management and consensus both frame **FY27 as a reset year** (flat ~16% EBITDA margin, EPS +10% that's
partly buyback). So from now (Nov) to the **April Q3 FY27 print**, this can be **range-bound / dead
money** — exactly the "opportunity-cost position" you said you don't want to repeat.

So the answer to "is it time" is: **it's time to START, not time to back up the truck.**

### Recommended structure (your own barbell, applied)
- **Start a small starter now — ~1/3 of target size.** Justification: the de-rate already happened,
  you're ~5% off a 3-month floor, downside is cushioned (backlog + buyback + 5.6% FCF yield), and a
  starter anchors you so you're not watching from the sidelines if the Jan print pops it.
- **Add #1 — late Jan (Q2 FY27 print):** the first confirmation. Mgmt promised **service revenue growth
  doubles in Q2.** If it does (and backlog holds >$2B), add to ~2/3.
- **Add #2 — late April (Q3 FY27 print):** THE inflection print. If revenue + Security margin visibly
  turn up, complete the position.
- **Break-it rule:** a close **below ~$186 on heavy volume**, especially after a Q2 that shows service
  growth *not* re-accelerating → the floor failed and FY27-reset is becoming FY27-stall. Stop adding,
  reassess (that's the Landis/Adobe discipline).

**Why not just watchlist-and-wait for April?** You can — it's the lower-regret path and you'd pay up
only ~15-20% if it works. But you give up the floor entry and the 5.6% yield, and you risk chasing. A
**1/3 starter splits the difference**: real skin in, sized so being wrong on timing doesn't hurt.

**Why not full position now?** Because FY27 is explicitly a reset and the real catalyst is 2 quarters+
out — full size now is the dead-money risk you flagged.

**Net: small starter (~1/3) now, scale on the Jan and April prints, break below $186. It's a BUY, sized
for a semi-dated catalyst — not a table-pound.**
