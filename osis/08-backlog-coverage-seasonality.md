## Coverage ratio (backlog ÷ LTM revenue) — the key signal
- Sat flat at **~1.05–1.08x for six straight quarters**, then **jumped to 1.16x in Q1 FY27** — a
  multi-year high.
- **Why it matters:** coverage = how much forward revenue is already contracted. Rising coverage means
  **backlog is growing FASTER than revenue** → forward visibility is *improving*, not just holding.
  1.16x = ~14 months of revenue already in hand. For a lumpy government-contract business, **>1x and
  rising is a genuinely strong, objective signal** — it's the quantitative proof the backlog story is
  real, independent of management's words. This is the single best "floor is strengthening" data point.
- The jump was driven by the **1.4 book-to-bill** in Q1 FY27 (the $583M bookings quarter).

## Book-to-bill read
Consistently ≥1.0 (orders ≥ revenue → backlog builds). The **1.4 in Q1 FY27** is the standout. The
**0.98 in Q4 FY26** is NOT a bad quarter — Q4 is the seasonally *largest* revenue quarter, so booking
above 1x against that high base is hard, and Middle East booking deferrals weighed. Backlog still held
$1.9B. Context matters: a sub-1 in the biggest quarter ≠ weakness; a 1.4 in the smallest quarter = real strength.

## Security revenue — estimate beat history (sandbag check) [LSEG file 9]
OSI **beats Security revenue estimates ~85% of the time (11 of last 13 quarters)**, typically +2–8%.
The only 2 misses: Q3 FY26 (−1.8%) and **Q4 FY26 (−12%, the Middle East slip)**. Q1 FY27 was back to
beating (+2.4%). → **Management/analysts run conservative; the company usually clears the bar.**

**Forward Security bar (the sandbag):**
- **Q2 FY27 (Dec-26): est $326M = −2% YoY** → a LOW bar (matches "Q2 modestly down" guide) → **beat likely.**
- **Q3 FY27 (Mar-27): est $355M = +11% YoY** → the 2H ramp is BAKED IN (not sandbagged — this is the expectation).
- **Q4 FY27 (Jun-27): est $392M = +15% YoY** → recovery from the slipped quarter, ramp baked in.
- FY27 Security est $1,331M = +7% vs FY26.

**Read:** Q2 is set low → a beat there is probable (85% base rate) but on an easy comp, so it's weak
evidence. **The real test is Q3/Q4, where +11%/+15% is already the bar** — hitting it = in line (no
surprise), beating it = the bull catalyst, missing it = thesis-stall. Only **2 analysts cover it**
(Oppenheimer, Roth) — thin coverage = more room for an edge, but also less scrutiny.

## Forward quarterly estimates — the 2H ramp, decomposed [LSEG files 10-12]
Consolidated revenue estimate vs prior-year actual (the bar):
| Quarter | Est | YoY | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q2 FY27 (Jan) | $452M | **−2.5%** | easy bar (down YoY) — a beat here is pre-loaded, weak evidence |
| Q3 FY27 (Apr) | $498M | **+9.8%** | THE ramp — not sandbagged, this is the expectation |
| Q4 FY27 | $549M | **+13.4%** | ramp (recovery off the slipped Q4 FY26) |

**The entire ramp is Security** (Q3 +11%, Q4 +15%); Opto steady (~+5%), Healthcare modest recovery (~+5%).
So the 2H bet = **Security converting backlog.** What the guide assumes: ~$30M deferred Middle East
converts in 2H, Mexico headwind 1H-only, CBP small in FY27 (bulk FY28), service growth accelerating.

**What would stop them:** Middle East slips again (the Q4 FY26 −12% miss was exactly this, #1 risk) ·
gov shutdown/procurement delay · site/installation delays (GAO bottleneck) · bad-margin mix (hit
revenue, miss margin — the Q1 problem).

**Seasonality share:** Q1 ~21% / Q2 ~25% / Q3 ~25% / Q4 ~28% of annual. Read each quarter vs its
seasonal share + YoY, never vs the prior quarter. The 2H carries both the seasonal bulge AND the
backlog conversion AND the operating leverage.

## Inventory composition — a tangible leading indicator of the 2H ramp (Q1 FY27 10-Q)
| Component | Jun 2026 | Sep 2026 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Raw materials** | $262M | $298M | **+$36M (+14%)** |
| **Work-in-process** | $77M | $87M | **+$10M (+12%)** |
| Finished goods | $77M | $79M | +$2M (+3%) |
| Total | $416M | $463M | +$48M (+11%) |

**The $48M build was ~entirely raw materials + WIP; finished goods barely moved.** That's the BULLISH
kind of inventory build: they're buying inputs and starting production to SHIP the backlog in 2H — not
piling up unsold scanners. Corroborates mgmt's "inventory built to support 2H scheduled deliveries from
backlog." A balance-sheet signal that conversion is being physically prepared. WATCH: raw/WIP continuing
to build into Q2 = ramp on track; finished goods ballooning = something's not shipping.
