## FY2026 (ended June 30, 2026) — the last full year
- **Revenue: $1.79B**, +4% YoY (finished *below* guide — ~$50M of Security deliveries slipped past
  June 30 due to Middle East site-access/logistics; **deferred, not lost** — stayed in backlog).
- **Non-GAAP EPS: $10.35**, +11% YoY (record).
- **Operating cash flow: $276M** (record); Q4 alone was a record **$182M** on big Mexico collections.
- **Backlog: ~$1.9B** (record at the time).
- Full-year **service revenue $441M, +13%**.

### FY26 revenue by division (approx, from the calls)
- Security ~70% of total. Opto **$451M (+9%)**. Healthcare smallest, improving late in year.

**Segment operating margins Q1 FY27 (non-GAAP):** Security **12.6%**, Opto **12.1%**,
Healthcare **–1.8%** (the loss is what dragged the consolidated margin).

**Honesty flag from the CFO (own words):** a meaningful part of the EPS growth came **below the
operating line** — lower net interest expense (~$3.3M vs $7.4M) and a **lower share count (16.3M
diluted vs 17.5M, –7%)** from buybacks. So Q1 EPS beat was partly financial engineering, not pure
operational. Margins were *down* YoY. Keep that in mind — see thesis doc.

## FY2027 guidance (raised at Q1, Oct 29)
- **Revenue reaffirmed: $1.875B – $1.930B** (≈ +5% to +8% YoY).
- **Non-GAAP EPS raised to $11.28 – $11.64** (≈ +9% to +12.5%) — *the raise was almost entirely
  from Q1 buybacks, not improved operations* (mgmt confirmed this explicitly).
- **Shape:** growth **weighted to 2H FY27**. Q2 revenue expected **modestly down YoY** (toughest
  Middle East comp + Mexico headwind). 2H carries the backlog conversion + service acceleration.
- **FCF > net income** for the full year (strong Mexico collections continue).

## Balance sheet (Sep 30, 2026)
- Cash $236.3M + ST investments $65.7M = **~$302M liquidity**; nothing drawn on the revolver.
- Total debt **~$1.0B** (>90% fixed rate). Convertibles: **2.25% notes due 2029** (conversion
  **$191.98** — *note: stock is near this, so dilution/settlement matters*) and **0.50% notes due
  2031** (conversion $353.82).
- Accounts receivable **$746.9M**, Inventory **$463.4M** (built +$48M in Q1 for 2H deliveries).
- Goodwill $407.6M, total equity $782.8M.
- **Mexico receivable:** was ~$190M (25% of total AR) at June 2026, collecting down through FY27 —
  this is the engine of the "FCF > net income" story.

## Capital allocation
- **Buybacks are the main lever right now.** Q1 FY27: repurchased ~306k shares at ~$204 (~$62.8M).
  FY26: repurchased 1.1M shares. Board added 1M to authorization in Aug; ~773k remained after Q1.
  Share count is steadily falling — a real EPS tailwind at these prices.
- No dividend.
- Priorities (mgmt): organic investment → strategic M&A → opportunistic buybacks → strong balance
  sheet. "Lots of dry powder" for acquisitions (RF deal is the template).
