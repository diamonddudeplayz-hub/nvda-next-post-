## What the company is
OSI Systems (NASDAQ: OSIS, ~$3.1B market cap, ~$3.9B EV) makes X-ray/scanning systems for security,
plus optoelectronic components and hospital patient-monitoring. Three segments:
- **Security ~70%** (brand: Rapiscan/AS&E). **#1 globally in CARGO/VEHICLE/BORDER inspection (>50%
  share)**; only #2–3 in airport passenger screening (behind Smiths Detection). Includes a growing
  **RF/defense-radar** line now plugged into the US "Golden Dome" missile-defense program ($235M award).
- **Optoelectronics ~20%** — sensors/components to Fortune 500 OEMs + supplies OSI's own products
  (vertical integration).
- **Healthcare <10%** (Spacelabs) — patient monitoring; currently loss-making, recovery optionality.

**Moat:** regulatory certification (TSA/ECAC: 3–5 yrs + millions per machine type to enter), software/
operator lock-in, cargo-scale dominance, and a razor/blade service annuity (10–15 yr machine life).
Mandated by regulation, AI-disruption-resistant (can't software away physical screening).

## The thesis (why now)
1. **It de-rated ~37% (forward P/E ~27x → ~17x)** from the April 2026 peak ($311) to ~$196 — not
   because the business broke, but because it was priced for perfection (34x trailing) and delivered
   "fine": service growth decelerated to ~5%, Middle East conflict slipped ~$50M of deliveries
   (deferred, not lost), and a US gov shutdown delayed orders. The multiple came out.
2. **Record $2.1B backlog with RISING coverage (backlog/LTM rev 1.16x, a multi-year high vs ~1.05x),
   book-to-bill 1.4.** Objective proof of forward demand.
3. **FY28/29 funding is legally LOCKED:** CBP has >$1B from the "One Big Beautiful Bill" for inspection
   systems (CBP Jan-2026 testimony), and the Secure America Act (signed June 2026) locks ~$26B CBP
   funding through FY2029 via reconciliation — "Democrat-proof" even after the Nov-2026 midterms. OSI
   is **sole-sourced** on recent CBP IDIQs ($200M + $85M ceilings) and historically wins >40% share.
4. **Reverse-DCF:** at $196 the market prices only **~4% perpetual FCF growth** for a 6–9% revenue
   grower with locked funding — i.e. it's pricing the pessimistic "reset, no re-acceleration" case.
5. **Dated catalysts are close:** Q2 FY27 (~27 Jan 2027, service growth should double), Q3 FY27
   (~Apr, THE 2H-ramp print), FY28 revenue ramp from ~mid-2027.
6. **Capital allocation:** buying back stock with cash (not debt) at ~$204 (above today) — management
   signaling it's cheap.

## The honest bear case
Lumpy, government-timing-dependent hardware company whose **gross margin has FALLEN from ~36% (2017)
to ~33% (FY26)** (segment mix: more low-margin Opto + Mexico), carries ~2.6x leverage (vs peer Smiths'
net cash), whose "2H inflection" is a *promise* that has slipped twice, whose biggest growth driver is
a year+ out, and whose "software/service" story is **thinner than bulls imply** (service is ~25% of
revenue growing ~1pt/yr — analysts model margins FLAT, not expanding). **The entire return depends on
the multiple re-rating (SOTP ≈ spot).** If the April print disappoints, it de-rates to ~14x (~$160,
the Aug low / 200-week line). Risk = dead money or −18%.

## Key risks to monitor
1. **2H FY27 ramp doesn't show (April print)** — the single biggest tell. 2. **Gross margin keeps
eroding.** 3. **Middle East re-escalates** → more slips. 4. **CBP procurement stalls** (GAO flagged
unused scanners in storage). 5. **Leverage** 2.6x (fine but not a fortress). 6. **Convertible dilution**
at $191.98. 7. **It's a lumpy gov contractor** — deserves *some* discount.
