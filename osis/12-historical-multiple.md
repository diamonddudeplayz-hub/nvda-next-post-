## What the data proves
1. **27x is NOT a peak anomaly.** OSIS traded 25–32x in 2013, 2016, 2017, 2019, 2025 — repeatedly,
   across cycles. Its **10-yr MEDIAN is ~21x.** The bear's "it deserves 13–15x, market finally figured
   it out" **fails the data** — the market has a demonstrated, decade-long willingness to pay 20x+.
2. **So a re-rate 17x → 20–21x is reversion to the MEDIAN, not a stretch.** The re-rate leg is
   empirically grounded, not hopeful. This is the strongest single fact for the bull.
3. **BUT the troughs are real and also grounded:** 12–14x in 2020 (Covid) and 2022 (rate shock). In a
   genuine drawdown or if the ramp fails, **13–15x is where OSIS actually bottoms.** The bear's downside
   multiple isn't fantasy — it's the historical floor.
4. **Caveat on "cheap":** on *trailing* EPS, today's ~21x is AT the median — fair, not cheap. The
   cheapness is only on *forward* (17x). And the P/E is noisy because EPS is the denominator (FY16 EPS
   dip spiked P/E to 37 mechanically; FY24 EPS jump compressed it). Read the band, not any single point.

## The reframe (supersedes the earlier "undated re-rate may never come")
The re-rate is **NOT hypothetical** — OSIS's own history shows the market pays ~20–32x for this business
when the narrative works, median ~21x. The question isn't "will the market ever pay up?" (it reliably
does) — it's **"will the 2H ramp re-prove the narrative enough to earn the median multiple again?"** —
a narrower, dated question the Jan/Apr prints answer. Underwriting to **20x (below the 21x median, well
below the 25–32x upper range)** is the *conservative* choice within the demonstrated band, not an
aggressive one.

**Net: entry ~17x fwd (≈median on trailing) · realistic re-rate to 20–25x (historically normal) ·
historical trough 12–14x (the real downside). Funded backlog skews odds away from the trough (those
were macro/EPS-collapse events); median-plus re-rate is the base case if the ramp converts.**

### The honest conclusion (corrects "it's cheap, 27x proves it")
- **On forward, today's 17x ≈ the 18x median → you're at the MEDIAN, NOT at a discount to history.** The
  "cheap" feeling is only vs the 2024-25 froth (22-27x), not vs how OSIS normally trades forward.
- **27x forward was the PEAK (2015/2025), not normal.** Median is 18x. Underwrite the re-rate to **20x**
  (above median, below peak) — conservative within the range.
- **The re-rate IS real** (hit 24-27x+ in 2012/15/16/25 when growth was visible) — so a move to 20-22x is
  the upper-half, not a stretch. But it needs the narrative (April ramp) to reignite.
- **You are NOT buying a cheap multiple.** The margin of safety is the BUSINESS (funded backlog, RPO,
  FCF yield, floor), NOT the valuation. Multiple is fair/neutral both ways.
- **Downside is real & symmetric-ish:** forward trough 11-14x (2020/22/23) = −20% to −35% multiple hit
  from here in a macro risk-off or failed ramp.

**Net: entry at the median forward multiple (fair, not cheap) · return = EPS growth + re-rate to the
20-25x upper range IF the April ramp reignites the narrative · downside = 11-14x trough in a bad
scenario · the multiple is neutral — the floor is the business, not the price.**
