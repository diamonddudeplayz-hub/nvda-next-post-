# OSIS — Sell-Side Analyst Notes (summaries)

*Summaries of the four analyst PDFs filed for OSIS. Street is Buy-skewed; the one bear (JPM) is the
most useful for the "what breaks it" view. Consensus PT ~$266 vs price ~$196 (~35% upside).*

---

## Oppenheimer — Oct 30, 2026 — **Outperform, PT $265** (post Q1 FY27)
*"Backlog Ramps; FY2H Growth/Mix Inflection Close at Hand"*
- **Yes — the inflection IS their explicit call.** What they actually say (quoted):
  - *"OSI continues to work through a softer organic stretch reflecting 2H-weighted backlog
    conversion cadence and easier comps. We anticipate **+11% FY2H27 organic growth** (and modestly
    favorable interest), net of softer FY1H mix trends."*
  - **$583M bookings / 1.4x book-to-bill** in Q1 → record $2.1B backlog = the fuel for the ramp.
  - Driver = **services growth supporting margin upside at Security**; they model **FY2H Security
    margins flat y/y** (following FY1H mix pressure), with net tailwinds from RF + services outgrowth.
- **But they're honest about the Q1 miss on mix** (don't gloss this): Q1 **adj. operating margin
  9.5% contracted 80bps y/y**, and **Security op margin 12.6% came in BELOW their 14.0% estimate**
  ("reflecting less favorable revenue mix"). So even the lead bull saw Q1 margins disappoint — the
  bull case rests entirely on the **2H** turn, not on anything that's printed yet.
- Takeaway: Oppenheimer's "inflection close at hand" = **2H FY27 (Jan→Apr prints), +11% organic,
  services-mix-driven margin recovery.** It is a *forecast* of the turn, explicitly "close at hand"
  but not yet in the numbers — which is exactly our "semi-dated catalyst" read.
- Quarterly non-GAAP EPS anchors: FY26 Q1 1.42 / Q3 2.58; FY27 Q1 1.66A / Q2E ~2.53.

## Oppenheimer — Sep 23, 2026 — **Outperform** (pre-Q1)
*"Banner Projects Win Streak Anchors Long-Term Compounding"*
- Focus on the **win streak**: CBP IDIQs, RF/Golden Dome $235M, SHIELD — argues these "banner
  projects" underwrite multi-year compounding even if FY27 is a transition year.
- Long-term-compounding framing (not a near-term catalyst call).

## Roth (Roth MKM) — Oct 30, 2026 — **Buy, PT $274** (post Q1 FY27)
*"Bookings Steal the Show in 1Q27"*
- Point: the quarter's story is **bookings/backlog (1.4 book-to-bill, $2.1B)**, not the P&L.
- Revenue model ($M): FY25A 344/420/444/505; FY26A 385/464/453/484; **FY27E 403.5A/451/504/~570(impl)**;
  FY28E 399/465/504/… → the 2H FY27 ramp is explicit.
- Cash $302M, total debt $999.6M.

## JPMorgan — Aug 28, 2026 — **Neutral, PT $250** (post Q4/FY27-guide) — THE BEAR
*"Improving Cash Collection, but FY27 Sales, Margin Forecasts Below Expectations, Acceleration
Expected Beyond"*
- The clearest statement of the bear/skeptic case, and it's not really bearish — it's "good company,
  wrong year": **FY27 guide below Street, FY27 = margin reset**, real acceleration is **FY28/FY29**.
- Mechanics: of the ~$50M delayed Middle East deliveries, **~60% (~$30M) converts in Q3/Q4 FY27**,
  ~$20M into FY28; ~$25M Mexico headwind concentrated in 1H. Note: Middle East rev *still grew* to
  ~$75M in FY26 from ~$50M FY25 despite the conflict.
- Services growth + RF demand support near-term; CBP + Golden Dome drive FY28/29 visibility.
- **PT $250 = 18x CY28E EPS of $13.80.** (Lowered target date Dec-26 → Dec-27; prior PT $270.)

## Read-across for our thesis
- **Everyone agrees on the shape:** FY27 is a transition/reset year weighted to 2H; the real
  acceleration is **FY28/29** on CBP (BBB ~$1B+) + RF/Golden Dome backlog conversion.
- **The bull/bear split is purely about *when*, and what multiple to pay for the wait.** Bulls
  ($265-300) pay now for the backlog; the bear ($250, Neutral) wants to see 2H FY27 convert first.
- **This validates our "semi-dated catalyst" read** — the forcing function is backlog + the 2H FY27
  prints (esp. the **April Q3 print**), not an inflection already in the numbers. JPM's $13.80
  CY28E EPS @ 18x is the cleanest anchor for a re-rate target if conversion plays out.
