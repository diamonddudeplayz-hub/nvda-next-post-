## The honest tension: is the catalyst HARD or SOFT?

Your governing rule (the Adobe lesson): **cheap + a *dated forcing function* beats cheap + a
sentiment catalyst.** Where does OSIS actually sit?

**The HARD parts (dated, in-hand):**
- **Record $2.1B backlog, book-to-bill 1.4** — this is contracted, not hoped-for.
- **Dated prints** force the issue: Q2 FY27 (~late Jan 2027), Q3 (~late Apr), Q4 (~Aug). Management
  has explicitly said **2H is where the ramp shows**. So by the April print you *know* if backlog
  is converting.
- **RF/Golden Dome $235M award** is already **being recognized as revenue now** (CFO: "as we speak").
- **Big Beautiful Bill ~$1B+ NII funding** is *appropriated* — the money exists; it's a question of
  release timing, not whether it's coming.

**The SOFT parts (be honest):**
- The big US re-rate (CBP IDIQs: $200M + $85M ceilings, ~$1B+ BBB) is mostly **FY28/29 revenue**, not
  FY27. Management keeps repeating "portion in '27, vast majority in '28 and beyond." So the *large*
  catalyst is a year+ out.
- Q1's EPS beat was **buyback + lower interest, not operations** — margins were *down* YoY. The
  guide raise was buyback-driven. So the "inflection" hasn't started in the margin line yet.
- Middle East is a live overhang that can keep slipping deliveries (it already did, twice).

**My call, no hedge:** This is a **"semi-dated" catalyst — better than Adobe, not as clean as
Itron.** The forcing function is real (backlog + the 2H prints), but you're being asked to trust
backlog *conversion* on a 2H-FY27-into-FY28 calendar, with Middle East as the wildcard. The entry
is attractive *because* the stock already de-rated to ~17x — you're not paying for the inflection,
you're being paid to wait (FCF, buyback, backlog floor). **It clears your bar, but it's a
"backlog-conversion" trade, not an "earnings-already-printing" trade. Size it like Xylem, not like
a table-pound.**

## The sell-off autopsy — what happened on the chart

You flagged a "massive drop from 276 → 234." Here's the precise forensics from the daily data:

### Drop #1 — the big one: **May 5, 2026 (−17% in a day)**
- **May 4 close $282.87 → May 5 close $234.74.** Intraday May 5 opened $276.89 and cratered to a
  $232.50 low. (You remembered "276→234" — that's this move; it was May 5, not May 12.)
- **Trigger: Q3 FY26 earnings, reported May 4 after close.** On paper it was a *record* quarter
  (rev $453M, EPS $2.60, record $1.9B backlog). So why the crash? Three things the market hated:
  1. **Service revenue growth decelerated to ~5%** (mid-single-digit) after *four straight quarters
     of strong double-digits.* Service is the entire margin-expansion thesis — the high-margin
     recurring line. A blip there, on a stock priced at ~34x for perfection, broke the narrative.
  2. **Middle East conflict** began hitting in the back half of Q3 — first signs of delivery/booking
     delays.
  3. **DHS government shutdown** delayed US procurement. Guidance was *maintained, not raised.*
- Translation: **a priced-for-perfection momentum stock got a "the flywheel might be stalling"
  quarter + two fresh overhangs → violent multiple reset.** Classic sell-the-news on a record print.

### Drop #2 — the confirmation: **Aug 21, 2026 (−17% intraday capitulation)**
- Aug 20 close $218.09 → Aug 21 traded down to **$186.50** (the 52-wk low) before closing $206.73
  on **1.29M volume** (5-6x normal). **Trigger: Q4 FY26 earnings (Aug 20).** FY26 revenue finished
  *below guide* (the ~$50M Middle East slip), and **initial FY27 guidance (rev +5-8%, EPS +7.5-11%)
  came in below Street** — JPMorgan's note title literally reads *"FY27 Sales, Margin Forecasts
  Below Expectations."* FY27 was framed as a **margin-reset year** with acceleration pushed to FY28.

### The through-line
The stock went from ~$311 (April peak) to ~$186 (Aug low) — a **~40% de-rate** — not because the
business broke, but because it was **priced for flawless double-digit compounding and instead
delivered a year of "deferred, not lost" + "acceleration is next year."** The multiple went from
~34x to ~17x. **That de-rate is the opportunity** — *if* you believe the backlog converts in 2H
FY27/FY28. The market has fully stopped paying for the inflection. You're buying the reset.

## What would make me WRONG (break conditions — the bear case, straight)
1. **Q3 FY27 (April) 2H ramp doesn't show up** — revenue stays flat, service growth stays single-
   digit, Security margin doesn't expand. That kills the "inflection on a calendar" and makes it a
   value trap with a story. **This is the single most important thing to watch.**
2. **Middle East re-escalates** and slips more deliveries (mgmt flagged possible escalation after
   US midterms). More "deferred not lost" = dead money.
3. **CBP / BBB money stalls** in procurement (another shutdown, reconciliation fight) — the FY28
   acceleration gets pushed again.
4. **Margins keep leaking** — if EPS growth stays dependent on buybacks + lower interest while
   operating margins decline, the quality thesis is hollow. Watch segment operating margins, not EPS.
5. **Convertible dilution** — 2029 notes convert at **$191.98**; stock is right there. Understand
   the share-count/cash-settlement mechanics before sizing (10-Q Note 8).
6. **Healthcare stays in the red** longer than expected — small, but it's the current drag.
