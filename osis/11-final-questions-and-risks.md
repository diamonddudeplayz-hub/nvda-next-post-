# OSIS — Final Questions, Open Risks & What To Monitor

*The due-diligence gaps and the honest risk list. These are the questions a sharp investor asks before
and during the hold.*

## The 6 questions I'd still want answered (DD gaps)
1. **What's the REAL organic growth, ex-Mexico and ex-RF-acquisition?** The headline growth is flattered
   by the Mexico super-cycle and the RF deal. Strip both out — is the *core* detection business growing
   mid-single-digit, or barely? (This tells you if the moat is compounding or just milking contracts.)
2. **How much of the $2.1B backlog is Middle East (at-risk) vs US (locked)?** The coverage ratio is only
   as good as the backlog's convertibility. If a big chunk is stuck-in-a-warzone Middle East, 1.16x
   coverage is softer than it looks. (10-K/10-Q geographic backlog disclosure; ask IR.)
3. **What's the service revenue gross margin exactly, and how big is the installed base driving the
   warranty roll-off?** The whole "service inflection" rests on this. Quantify the installed base and
   the warranty-expiry schedule — that's the verifiable engine.
4. **Is RF/Golden Dome durable or a one-contract wonder?** $235M was huge, but is there a *pipeline* of
   follow-ons, or did they win the one big thing? (Mgmt says SHIELD IDIQ = more; verify with awards.)
5. **Are management good capital allocators?** The RF deal was a "grand slam" — is that repeatable skill
   or luck? Track record on M&A + buyback timing. (They're buying at ~$204 now — good sign.)
6. **Insider ownership / alignment?** Who owns it, are insiders buying, is comp aligned. (Thin coverage
   = more room for an edge, but also less scrutiny.)

## The honest bear case in one paragraph
OSIS is a lumpy, government-timing-dependent hardware company whose gross margin has quietly *fallen*
for five years, carrying ~2.6x leverage, whose "inflection" is a 2H-FY27 *promise* that's slipped
before, whose biggest growth driver is a year+ out, and whose "software/service" story is thinner than
the bulls imply. If the Apr print disappoints and the multiple de-rates to where lumpy defense-services
names trade (~14x), you're looking at ~$160 (−18%) back to the Aug low / 200-week. **That's the risk
you're taking.**

## The honest bull case in one paragraph
A regulation-moated #1-in-cargo-inspection business, de-rated ~37% (27x→17x forward) to where the
market prices only ~4% growth, sitting on a record $2.1B backlog with *rising* coverage (1.16x), with
FY28/29 funding now *legally locked* through 2029 (Secure America Act + OBBBA $1B+), buying back stock
with real cash at prices above today, near a 3-month floor and its 200-week line, with dated catalysts
only 3–6 months out (Jan/Apr prints) and the big revenue ramp ~mid-2027. If it just re-rates back toward
its own recent norm (20–24x) as the backlog converts, that's +45% to +65%. **You're paid ~5.6% FCF
yield to wait, with a visible floor.**

## Where I land (and where YOU might be too bullish)
- **It's a legitimate, well-supported starter → scale-in.** EV ~+16%, asymmetric (+45%/−18%), with a
  real floor and locked long-term funding.
- **Your overly-bullish risk:** the *entire* return rides on the multiple re-rating (SOTP base ≈ today's
  price — you're NOT getting cheap assets, you're betting on 17x→20x+). It HAS traded there before, so
  it's a reasonable bet — but if the market stays sour (gov lumpiness, flat margins), you can sit at
  17x for a year earning ~the FCF yield and little else. **That's the "dead money until it re-rates"
  risk — same as your Itron.** Size for that: it's a *patient* re-rate, not a fast one.
- **Discipline:** don't pay up past ~$230 (base) chasing; add on weakness toward $177; the asymmetry is
  best near the floor, worst near the target.
