# OSIS — Q3 FY2026 Earnings Call — May 4, 2026

*Structured notes. Quarter ended Mar 31, 2026. Stock reaction: **May 5 crash −17%
($282.87 → $234.74)** — this is "Drop #1," the big one, in the thesis doc. A record quarter that
the market sold hard. Understand WHY — it's the key to the whole setup.*

## Headline
- Revenue **$453M** (Q3 record). Non-GAAP EPS **$2.60** (Q3 record). Backlog record **~$1.9B**.
- **Book-to-bill 1.3**. OCF $14M (light — limited Mexico collections in qtr; collected ~$74M just
  after quarter end).
- Security rev $319M. **Ex-Mexico, Security grew +25% YoY** (Mexico fell to $11M from $69M PY).
- Opto +10% to $111M (Q3 record). Healthcare soft (US order timing → lower sales/profit).

## WHY THE STOCK CRASHED (the three things the market hated)
1. **Service revenue growth decelerated to ~5%** (mid-single digit) after **4 straight quarters of
   strong double-digit** growth. Service is the entire margin-expansion thesis. Analyst Chris Glynn
   (Oppenheimer) pressed hard: "should we view it as stepping down to single-digit going forward?"
   Mgmt: no, timing/comp, expect strong service growth to resume — but the doubt was planted.
2. **Middle East conflict** began impacting in "the latter half of Q3" — delivery delays, logistics,
   travel restrictions. First appearance of the overhang that would dog the next two quarters.
3. **DHS government shutdown** delayed US procurement (CBP orders). Guidance **maintained, not raised.**
- Context: stock was ~$283 at ~34x NTM, **priced for perfection**. A record quarter with a
  flywheel wobble + two fresh overhangs = violent multiple reset. Textbook sell-the-news.

## Key disclosures

  business acquired ~18 mo earlier).

- Opto: $40M medical-OEM award (big vs usual <$5M orders); book-to-bill well >1.
- Mexico receivable ~$345M at Mar 31; ~$74M collected right after quarter end.
- Adj op margin 14%. Security adj op margin 18.3%.

## The lesson for the thesis
This quarter is the origin of the de-rate. The business kept setting records on **product/backlog**,
but the market cares about the **service-margin inflection** — and the moment that looked shaky,
plus geopolitics, the ~34x multiple was indefensible. The stock has since fallen to ~17x. The
bull case is that the service deceleration was genuinely timing (mgmt insists Q2 FY27 service
growth doubles), and the backlog converts in 2H FY27. The bear case is the flywheel is just slower
than the multiple assumed.
