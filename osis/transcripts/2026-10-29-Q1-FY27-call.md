# OSIS — Q1 FY2027 Earnings Call — Oct 29, 2026

*Structured notes from the full transcript. Quarter ended Sep 30, 2026. Stock reaction: muted
(stock ~$208 into print, ~$196 by Nov 6 — no re-rate sustained).*

## Headline numbers
- Revenue **$403M, +5% YoY** (Q1 record). Products +5% to $290M; Services +4% to $114M.
- Non-GAAP EPS **$1.66, +17%** (Q1 record). GAAP diluted $1.51.
- Operating cash flow **$37M** (2x+ PY $17M); FCF ~$30M (~120% of GAAP NI).
- **Book-to-bill 1.4; backlog record ~$2.1B** (up from ~$1.9B at June 30).

## CFO's honesty flags (important)
- "A meaningful part of our EPS growth this quarter came **from below the operating line** — lower
  net interest expense and a lower share count." Net interest $3.3M vs $7.4M PY. Diluted shares
  16.3M vs 17.5M (**–7%**).
- Gross margin **30.7% vs 32.0%** — down. Driver: Healthcare revenue decline (highest-GM division),
  Opto strength (lowest GM), less favorable Security mix.
- Non-GAAP adj. operating margin **9.5% vs 10.3%**. Segment: Security 12.6%, Opto 12.1%,
  Healthcare **–1.8%** (loss).

## Division detail
- **Security** rev $264M, +4% (despite Middle East + Mexico transition headwind ~$8M in Q1, ~$11M
  expected Q2). Record Security backlog.
  - Two **5-yr CBP IDIQs**: ~$200M ceiling (relocatable Rapiscan passenger vehicle inspection) +
    ~$85M ceiling (van-mounted mobile X-ray). ~$21M initial delivery orders in hand. **Only ceiling-
    backed firm orders go into backlog, not ceiling values.**
  - **RF: $235M** over-the-horizon radar contract (largest RF award ever) **recognizing revenue
    now**; +~$90M more RF bookings in Q1. SHIELD IDIQ = Golden Dome vehicle.
  - **LA28 Olympics**: official screening provider + Team USA supporter.
- **Opto** rev $120M incl. intercompany, +9% (+16% to third parties). Record Q1, margin expansion.
- **Healthcare** rev **–14%** (patient monitoring weak, hospital capex deferral). Next-gen platform
  in development; expects return to growth.

## Guidance (FY27)
- **Revenue reaffirmed $1.875–1.930B** (+5% to +8%).
- **Non-GAAP EPS raised to $11.28–11.64** (+9% to +12.5%) — *raise driven by Q1 buybacks* (mgmt
  confirmed to analyst Larry Solow: "that would be a fair statement").
- Q2 revenue **modestly down YoY** (toughest Middle East comp + Mexico). **2H is the growth.**
- **Service growth: expect it to double+ in Q2**, accelerate further 2H. FCF > NI for the year.
