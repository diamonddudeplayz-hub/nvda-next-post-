# OSIS — BofA Industrials Conference Fireside (Alan Edrick, CFO) — May 12, 2026

*Structured notes. Best plain-English overview of the whole business — the CFO walks through it.
Held ~1 week after the Q3 crash, so useful color on the bull case at the reset.*

## Business shape (CFO's own framing)
- Three divisions, rough mix: **~70% Security, ~20%+ Opto, <10% Healthcare.**
- "We believe we're the **#1 security detection company in the world**" — aviation, ports/borders,
  critical infra, events.
- Opto = sensors/detectors/components, OEM supplier to Fortune 500 (aerospace/defense, medical,
  industrial) AND supplies components into OSI's own Security/Healthcare = **vertical integration**,
  margin captured in-house. "Started as an optoelectronics company," grew the rest on top.
- Healthcare = Spacelabs patient monitoring + cardiology; recurring via service/spares/consumables.

## Mexico (the overhang everyone asks about)
- 3 contracts totaling **>$800M** a few years ago (FY24-27 fulfillment). **No more material Mexico
  headwind in FY27** — transitions to high-margin service revenue.
- Receivable ~$350M at Mar; ~$70M+ collected after Q3; ~$270M remaining → **"extraordinary FCF over
  next 12 months."** Mexican federal govt (Army/Navy) "paid every single peso," just slowly. "If
  offered these contracts 10 more times, we'd take them 10 more times."

## Recurring revenue / margin thesis (the core)
- Service carries **>10 points higher margin** than product. Sell box w/ 1-yr warranty → rolls off →
  high-margin recurring service.
- **Recurring ≈ 30% of Security today; target 40%+.** "Couple top-line growth with operating margin
  expansion" — the decade-long formula.
- SaaS (CertScan) — remote screening adjudication; substantial margins.

## US opportunity (the FY28 acceleration)
- **One Big Beautiful Bill: >$1B for NII equipment** to CBP — "exactly what we do." Delayed by the
  shutdown. In past IDIQs OSI won **>40% share**; "preferred supplier" for CBP.
- Expects "very large orders." Secure America Act = potential +$2-3B more.

## RF / Golden Dome (the new leg)
- RF acquired ~18 months ago — "**a grand slam**," not a single/double. $235M OTH radar award;
  more expected. Capacity expanded **3-4x** (new facilities), can go to 2nd/3rd shift. Running
  ~$150M annualized (from much smaller at acquisition).

## Aviation (long-dated optionality)
- US **checked-baggage replacement cycle** starts "in a few years," lasts ~5 years. OSI has ~0% US
  installed base in checked-bag (like Europe before they took ~20-25% share) → share-gain + service
  tail opportunity. Competitive landscape (Smiths taken private; Leidos+Analogic merger) "doesn't
  change the landscape materially."

## Opto
- Normally mid-single-digit growth + margin expansion; lately double-digit on aerospace/defense
  strength + China supply-chain diversification. 1.5x book-to-bill. >2/3 produced outside US.

## Capital allocation
- Strong balance sheet, low net debt, big untapped revolver, lots of dry powder. FCF conversion
  normally ~100% of NI, next 12 months "nicely north of 100%."
- Can do organic + M&A + buybacks simultaneously.

## "What keeps you up at night"
- Things in their control: feel great. **Geopolitics** (Middle East) is the out-of-control variable.
