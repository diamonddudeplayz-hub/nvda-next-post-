## 3. How the search ran

1. **Universe.** 1,799 tickers: yesterday's 519, every US-listed company above $15B (756), and 39 more Yahoo industries covering midstream, contracted power, royalty and streaming, data and exchanges, services, healthcare and semis. 1,692 came back with data at $1B or more (`np2_universe.py`, `np2_resume.py`).
2. **Three quantitative cuts,** one per fingerprint, since the mechanisms don't look alike in a screener:
   - *Overhang (C):* down 30%+ from the five-year high, forward P/E under 18x, revenue not falling, profitable, net debt under 3.2x, three beats in four. 120 names.
   - *Contracted floor (B):* all 65 midstream, power, royalty and tower names, ranked on EV/EBITDA and yield.
   - *Pre-recognition (D):* remaining performance obligations from XBRL for all 1,692, latest against a year earlier (`np2_rpo.py`). 19 names have a book that is growing 15%+ and at least 8 points faster than revenue.
3. **Own-history check** on 72 survivors: ten-year trailing P/E and P/S bands from annual XBRL and weekly prices (`np2_history.py`). GAAP, so a screen only.
4. **Filing test on 34,** from the latest earnings release of each (`np2_release.py`).

### Hess Midstream (HESM), $30.92, $3.96B for the public Class A shares, debt $3.6B

**What it does.** Gathers and processes oil, gas and water in the Bakken, almost entirely for Chevron (which bought Hess in July 2025). From closing it adds Chevron's DJ Basin gathering system.

**Reported evidence (8-K of 8 Oct and Q3 release of 30 Oct 2026).**

Chevron drops from three rigs to two in December 2026.
- 2026 guide: adjusted EBITDA $1,225-1,250M, adjusted free cash flow $910-935M. 2027 preliminary: EBITDA $850-950M, capex $125M, free cash flow $525-625M, leverage 3.75-4.0x, distributions held at the Q4 2026 rate, Bakken volumes down about 5% then flat.

- Per share, on my arithmetic: EBITDA $6.00 in 2026 to $7.04 in 2027; free cash flow $4.47 to $4.50. The annual distribution at the expected Q4 rate is about $3.23, costing about $414M, covered 1.39x at the midpoint.
