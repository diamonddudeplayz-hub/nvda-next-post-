# AMZN at $247 — verdict and edge (Nov 22, 2026)

Built off the full set: 4 calls (Q4'25→Q3'26), 15 TIKR estimate files (20-yr history → 2030, incl.
segment revenue, AWS EBITDA, capex), the Q3'26 10-Q (EDGAR), and 4 Oct-30 sell-side notes. Price
anchor **$246.82 (Nov 20 close)**, −14% off the $287 high. Analysis, not advice. See the
`GOOGL-vs-AMZN-head-to-head.md` for the direct comparison.

## Verdict in five lines
1. **AWS is the single best AI-ROI engine in the complex right now** — $184B run-rate, **+39.3% and
   accelerating six straight quarters**, **38.7% operating margin**, backlog **$578B (+189% YoY)**,
   guided **>40% growth in BOTH 2027 and 2028**, "$1T revenue in 5–10 years." This is the cleanest
   proof that AI compute demand is real and monetizing at high margin.
2. **Amazon has a top-tier own-silicon franchise** — Trainium (Anthropic **and** OpenAI as multi-GW
   anchors, **$225B+ commitments**, selling externally late 2027) + Graviton (leading CPU). The custom-
   silicon business is ~$25B+ run-rate growing triple digits. This is the same "un-scarce the NVDA
   shovel" story people credit to Google's TPU — Amazon has it too.
3. **The retail + ads engine is a real, separate compounder** — op income **+61% YoY**, record 13.9%
   consolidated margin, NA/Intl margins +~10pts over a few years, and **Advertising ~$83B (26E) at high
   margin, growing ~20%.** Stores could be a second $1T revenue business.
4. **But it's not cheap and not very derated** — **23.0x FY27 clean EPS, −14% off the high, net debt
   ~$191B, beta 1.50.** Less margin of safety than GOOGL (17.8x, −35%, net cash). The FCF desert is
   real: consensus **−$36B (26) → −$48B (27) → +$5.5B (28)**, on ~$220B→$291B capex.
5. **The risk set is diffuse, not a single binary** — valuation, consumer/retail cyclicality (a
   recession hits Stores), net debt, and the largest absolute capex in the group. No single existential
   threat (AWS demand is backlog-floored and accelerating), but less cushion if the tape or the consumer
   turns.

## Where the price is underestimating (the bull edge)
- **AWS acceleration + the >40%/both-years guide is unusually specific** for Amazon, and backlog ($578B,
  "sold out through much of 2028") floors it. The Street was modeling ~41%; management is guiding *above*
  40% for two years — rare forward conviction. If AWS compounds >40%, the EV/EBITDA (9.9x FY27) is cheap.
- **AI margins are tracking core "a little ahead"** at a similar point of evolution — i.e., the feared
  "AI is dilutive" story is being disproven in AWS's own 38.7% margin, helped by Trainium (Amazon says
  own-silicon gives "several hundred bps of op-margin advantage" + "tens of billions of capex savings").
- **The retail/ads engine is under-credited** — a $83B high-margin ad business + a Stores segment with
  expanding margins is a second profit pool the "it's just a capex story" bears ignore.
- **FCF inflects a year earlier than GOOGL (2028)** and Amazon has done this capex→ROIC conversion before.

## Where the price might be right (be honest about the bear)
1. **Valuation leaves little margin of safety** — 23x FY27, only −14% off the high. If AWS q/q dollar
   growth keeps slowing (it decelerated to +$3.8B q/q from +$4.6B — a capacity/pricing wrinkle BofA
   flagged), the multiple has room to compress.
2. **Consumer/retail cyclicality** — ~60% of revenue is Stores; a recession or weak consumer hits
   Amazon in a way it doesn't hit a pure cloud/ads name. Tariffs, fuel/linehaul inflation are live
   cost pressures management is actively absorbing.
3. **Balance sheet + capex** — net debt ~$191B and the **largest absolute capex in the group** ($220B→
   $291B). The FCF desert is deeper than GOOGL's in dollar terms (−$48B 27E), and Amazon has been
   issuing debt to fund it.
4. **AWS margin will fluctuate** — management repeatedly says so; the 38.7% is partly capacity-
   optimization timing + a Q2 derivative gain, and AI mix is lower-margin than core. Don't extrapolate.
5. **GAAP EPS is distorted by equity marks** (Rivian/Anthropic): 9mo non-operating gain **+$68.4B**
   inflates FY26 EPS to $12.82; the clean forward is **FY27 $10.75**. Value on that + EV/EBITDA + FCF.

## What you need (the actionable read)
- **AMZN is the best AI *business* in the group, but the *entry* at $247 is only fair, not cheap.** It's
  −14% off its high with net debt and β 1.50 — so it's a **great name at a mediocre entry**, vs GOOGL (a
  great name at a good entry, −35%, net cash).
- **Own it — but as the "business-momentum" leg, added on weakness.** Wait for a flush toward the **low-
  $200s (~20x FY27)** before sizing up, or start smaller than GOOGL. Pairs well *with* GOOGL (hedges the
  Search risk) rather than instead of it.
- **Watch:** AWS q/q dollar growth (is the +$3.8B deceleration a blip or a trend?), AWS operating margin
  (sustain high-30s?), the 2027 capex number, retail margin + consumer health, and Trainium external-
  sales ramp (late 2027). _Sell trigger: AWS growth rolls back toward high-20s with no backlog offset,
  or retail margins reverse in a consumer downturn._

**Bottom line: AWS makes Amazon arguably the best AI-ROI business in the complex, and Trainium means the
own-silicon edge isn't Google's alone. But at 23x and −14% off the high with net debt, it's priced for
its quality — the margin of safety is in GOOGL, the momentum is in AMZN. Own both; start bigger in GOOGL,
add AMZN on a dip.**
