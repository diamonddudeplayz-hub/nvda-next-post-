Cash capex ~$75.9B in Q3 alone (management figure, net of incentives). **9mo FCF ≈ −$62B** — deeper in

## Management's capex/ROIC framing (from the calls, grounded here)
- **2026 cash capex ~$220B** (raised from ~$200B on memory-cost inflation), **"meaningfully more in 2027."**
- Two cycles: **data centers** (capital spent ~2 yrs before monetizing, then **30+ year** useful life,
  5–6 server generations) and **servers/networking** (bought months ahead, **<3-yr breakeven**, 5–6-yr
  life). "In high-growth periods capex outpaces revenue and FCF is challenged until capacity is
  monetized; then revenue outpaces capex and FCF/ROIC is very compelling." They've "done this before"
  in the first cloud era. This is the operating-leverage mechanism, stated by the company.
- **Trainium:** "several hundred bps of op-margin advantage + tens of billions of capex savings per year
  vs relying on others' chips." Backs the own-silicon margin thesis.

## Why this doc matters
The call sells the AWS story; the 10-Q confirms the financial reality: **record 13.9% consolidated
margin, AWS 38.7% at +39%, NA margin +82%** — but **9mo FCF ≈ −$62B and net debt ~$191B** funding the
largest capex in the complex. Amazon is spending and financing into the buildout as hard as anyone, with
a longer track record of making it pay. (Primary source: EDGAR, Amazon 10-Q, period 2026-09-30.)
