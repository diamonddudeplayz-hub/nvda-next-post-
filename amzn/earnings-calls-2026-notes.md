# Amazon earnings-call notes — Q4'25 → Q3'26 (the 4-quarter set)

Condensed notes from the four calls Darren pasted. The through-line: **AWS re-accelerating every
quarter, a top-tier own-silicon franchise (Trainium + Graviton), and a capex/ROIC defense.**

## Q3 2026 (Oct 29, 2026) — the one that matters
- Rev **$201.9B (+12%)**, op income **$28.1B (+61%)**, margin **13.9% (record)**.
- **AWS $46B, +39.3%** — "accelerating for the sixth straight quarter, fastest in ~5 years." $184B run-
  rate, **38.7% op margin**, backlog **$578B** (+triple digits). "Installing an AI accelerator every 14
  seconds."
- **Guide: AWS >40% growth in BOTH 2027 and 2028; $1T revenue business in 5–10 years.** "Very early days
  of this inflection."
- **Chips:** Trainium + Graviton revenue commitments **each +20x YoY**; customers saved >$2B in Q3 using
  custom silicon; **TorchNeuron** open-sourced (PyTorch on Trainium, no rewrite). **Will sell Trainium
  externally late 2027.** Bedrock: Fortune 500 spend +7x YoY, token volume +17x, "fastest-growing multi-
  billion-$ service in AWS history," added OpenAI models.
- **Stores:** double-digit-margin retail inclusive of ads; NA/Intl margins +~10pts over a few years;
  grocery/perishables inflecting; **Alexa for Shopping** 400M+ users (+570% interactions). Ads **$19.6B
  (+12%**, timing-noisy; expect high-teens Q4).
- **Capex ~$220B 2026, "meaningfully more in 2027."**

## Q2 2026 (Jul 30) — the acceleration + the capex/ROIC defense
- Rev $200.6B (+20%), op income $27.5B (+43%). **AWS +36.7%** (5th straight accel), $169B run-rate,
  backlog $496B. Chips run-rate **>$25B**, AI run-rate **>$25B**, both +triple digits.
- **Anthropic AND OpenAI** making "multiyear, multi-gigawatt Trainium commitments." Trainium2 ~30% better
  price-perf than GPUs, largely sold out; Trainium3 (+30–40%) nearly fully subscribed; Trainium4 reserved.
- **The ROIC framing (key):** data centers = capital 2 yrs before monetizing, then **30+ yr** life;
  servers = **<3-yr breakeven**, 5–6-yr life. "High growth → capex outpaces revenue → FCF challenged →
  then revenue outpaces capex → FCF/ROIC very compelling. We've done this before." **Capex raised to
  ~$220B** (from ~$200B) on memory costs. Ads **$19.8B (+26%)**.

## Q1 2026 (Apr 29) — Trainium scale revealed
- Rev $181.5B (+17%), op income $23.9B, margin 13.1% (then-record). **AWS +28%** (fastest in 15 qtrs),
  $150B run-rate. Backlog $364B (ex the >$100B Anthropic deal).
- **Chips run-rate >$20B; "if sold as a standalone merchant chip business, ~$50B run-rate — one of the
  top-3 data-center chip businesses in the world."** **>$225B of Trainium revenue commitments.** Trainium2
  ~30–40% better price-perf, sold out; Meta committed to "tens of millions of Graviton cores."
- **"At scale, Trainium saves us tens of billions of capex/yr and several hundred bps of op-margin
  advantage vs others' chips for inference."** Ads $17.2B (+22%). Rufus 300M+ users.

## Q4 2025 (Feb 5) — the setup
- Rev $213.4B (+12%), op income $25.0B, **TTM FCF $11.2B** (pre the 2026 negative-FCF era). **AWS +24%**
  ($142B run-rate), backlog **$244B (+40% YoY)**. AWS op margin 35% (Q4).
- Trainium2: 1.4M chips landed ("fastest-ramping chip launch ever"); **Project Rainier** = Anthropic
  training on Trainium2 (500k chips → scaling). **Capex ~$200B planned for 2026.** Added >1 GW capacity
  in Q4; added more data-center capacity than any company in 2025 (+3.9 GW TTM). Ads **$21.3B (+22%)**.

## The through-line for the thesis
Four quarters of **AWS acceleration (+24→+28→+37→+39%)** with **margin holding high-30s** and an
own-silicon business (Trainium/Graviton) that rivals Google's TPU — **both** AI labs as anchors, $225B+
commitments, merchant sales coming. The bear is **not demand** (backlog $578B, accelerating) — it's
**valuation, the capex/FCF desert, net debt, and consumer/retail cyclicality.** Management's entire
message is "once-in-a-lifetime, we can fund it, ROIC will be compelling, we've done this before."
