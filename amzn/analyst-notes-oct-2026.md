# AMZN — sell-side synthesis (Oct 30, 2026, post-Q3)

Four post-Q3 notes in the repo. **Uniformly bullish; the debate is q/q AWS dollar growth, not the thesis.**

- **JPMorgan — Overweight, PT $365** ("Almost Can't Help But Buy Amazon"). AWS +39% ($184B run-rate),
  slightly below their +41% est but "fastest in 5 years" and still capacity-constrained. Flags AWS **>40%
  in both '27 & '28** and **$1T revenue in 5–10 yrs** (Stores possibly reaching $1T too). Backlog **$578B
  (+189% YoY, +17% Q/Q)**; AWS "sold out through a good chunk of 2028."
- **BofA — Buy, PO $317** ("Cloud growth is lumpy, but still quite good"). Q3 rev $201.9B ≈ Street; GAAP
  profit $28.1B beat. AWS +39% accelerated 2ppts q/q, above Street's 38%. **Caveat:** q/q dollar growth
  slowed to **+$3.8B** (vs +$4.6B prior, below cloud peers) — "capacity limitations or client price
  adjustments." Backlog +$82B q/q offsets. "Building toward a $1T high-margin AWS opportunity."
- **Needham — Buy, PT $300, added to Conviction List** ("$1T/Year Revenue Streams Justify AMZN's CapX").
- **Morgan Stanley** (JP-language title) — constructive; "painful now, but trillions of good news ahead."

**Read:** Street PTs **$300–365** vs $247 spot → **~+21% to +48%** implied, unanimous Buy/OW. Nobody
disputes AWS demand; the one wrinkle (BofA) is the q/q dollar-growth deceleration — worth tracking as the
tell for whether +40% is capacity-constrained upside or early softening. Targets cluster above GOOGL's
implied upside in % terms, but AMZN starts from a shallower de-rate (−14% vs −35%).

_Sell-side; treat PTs as opinion, the backlog/margin data as the harder evidence._
