# Grid modernization — the Soros-book theme + the laggard screen

**Thesis:** the grid can't handle what AI + electrification is about to demand, so own the
power value chain. It's the "long AI's *demand* (electricity), disruption-proof in the
software sense, one step removed from chip cyclicality and SaaS disruption" leg of the barbell.
**Caveat held throughout:** a chunk of the *incremental* power-demand story IS the
datacenters, so the marquee power names share the datacenter-slowdown risk (they fell on
Nov 5). The part that's independent is **broad grid replacement / hardening / metering** —
which is where the edge is.

The single highest-conviction *theme* in the book is grid/power, funded by selling the shovels.

## Tiering by risk/reward (through the "catalyst + not-already-run" screen)
1. **Regulated utilities** (AEP, CMS, WEC, XEL, ETR, SRE, NEE): own the theme, but capped-ROE bond-proxies, rate-sensitive, and several already ran (ETR +1,084% = Soros *chasing*). Theme-safe, not asymmetric.
2. **Grid pick-and-shovel** (Itron + GE Vernova, Quanta, Vertiv, nVent, Powell, Eaton): the gear/software every utility must buy. **This is where the edge is — but most have already run.**
3. **Datacenter-power / nuclear** (DLR, X-energy, IPPs): highest beta to AI-power, most exposed to the slowdown, most already-priced.
