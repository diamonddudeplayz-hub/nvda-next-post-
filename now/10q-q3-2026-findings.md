# NOW Q3 FY26 10-Q — what's hidden / the trends that matter

Quarter ended Sep 30, 2026. Read cover-to-cover. The headline debate is cRPO (see
`q3-2026-and-crpo-bridge.md`); these are the things *underneath* the print.

## 1. GAAP vs non-GAAP is a chasm — know which one you're quoting
- **GAAP operating margin 9.4%** (op income $385M) vs the **"32.6%" non-GAAP** everyone cites. The ~$953M/qtr gap: **SBC $633M** (15.4% of revenue), **intangible amortization $198M**, **severance $87M**, M&A costs $35M.
- **GAAP net income only $277M** ($0.27 EPS); non-GAAP EPS ~$1.00. GAAP P/E is ~80x; the 22x everyone quotes is non-GAAP. The business is genuinely profitable on cash/non-GAAP, but GAAP profitability is thin and SBC is large.

## 2. THE key hidden trend: subscription gross margin is falling (80% → 74%)
- Subscription GM dropped from **80% to 74%** YoY, and management **guides it lower** for FY26 — explicitly on "ongoing growth of **third-party cloud services usage** (hyperscalers) and incremental **amortization of intangibles**."
- Translation: **AI is currently a COST (renting hyperscaler GPU compute to serve AI features) that hits the P&L BEFORE the AI revenue (assists) shows up.** This is the single most under-discussed tension. If assists monetize → GM recovers → big EPS lever. If assists lag → falling GM + decelerating cRPO = the bear thesis confirmed in the financials.

## 3. They levered up for M&A — goodwill is now 76% of equity
- **~$9.2B of acquisitions in 9 months:** Armis **$7.6B** (cyber-asset/OT security), Veza **$1.2B** (identity), +5 others $0.6B. (Plus Moveworks $2.4B, Logik.io $0.5B in late 2025.)
- Funded by a **$4.0B term loan** (bridge for Armis) + **$5.5B new senior notes** (May 2026, 4.25%–6.30%, due 2028–2056) + **$2.1B commercial paper**. Debt went **$1.5B → $7.5B** in a year.
- **Goodwill ballooned $3.6B → $10.0B** = 31% of total assets, **~76% of stockholders' equity ($13.1B)**. Intangibles $1.1B → $3.6B. **Impairment risk** if Armis/Veza underperform the "Security > all of ServiceNow" claim. Still modest leverage vs ~$106B cap and ~$6B FCF, but it's a real change in character.

## 4. Quality-of-earnings flag: $367M of private-investment markups in pretax income
- **Strategic investments $2.05B** (privately held companies, non-marketable equity, "measurement alternative"). They recorded **net upward adjustments of $367M** in 9mo flowing through *other income*.
- That $367M is **~25% of 9mo pretax income ($1,481M)** — non-cash markups of private (AI-ish) holdings inflating pretax income. The mirror image of Google's SpaceX *markdown*. Back it out for a cleaner earnings read.

## 5. Non-GAAP FCF adds back real cash M&A/legal costs
- Their FCF definition = GAAP operating cash flow **+ cash paid for legal settlements and business-combination costs** − capex. They added back **$371M** of M&A/legal cash costs in 9mo. So "36% FCF margin" is an *adjusted* figure; underlying is a touch lower. Minor, but know it.

## 6. Customer concentration + an active government investigation
- **One US federal channel partner = ~11% of total revenue and 20% of accounts receivable.** Federal is a top growth vector *and* the single biggest customer.
- **Legal (Note 18):** an active investigation by the **DOJ, DoD Office of Inspector General, and Army Suspension & Debarment Office** into a government contract — specifically the **hiring of a CIO**; the **President & COO and the hired individual resigned** (2024). Company is cooperating, "cannot predict timing, outcome or possible impact." A government-contract compliance probe while Federal is the concentration = real tail risk.

## 7. AI-driven layoffs — eating its own dogfood
- NEW 2026 accounting policy for **"workforce optimization through AI-driven efficiencies."** $167M severance 9mo; headcount cut (−1,602 in Q3). Internal AI generating >$1B annualized value. Margin tailwind — and a live datapoint on what AI does to software headcount generally.

## 8. "Capital return" is mostly dilution offset, not shrinkage
- SBC $1,846M (9mo) vs buybacks $2,225M (9mo, incl a $2.0B ASR in Q1 @ ~$108). Share count is **roughly flat** (~1,038M). $4.2B buyback authorization remains (~4% of the company at $102). The buyback neutralizes SBC rather than materially shrinking the share count.

## 9. Other
- **Interest expense $153M 9mo** (was $18M) — the new debt. Interest income falling ($227M, lower balances/rates).
- Revenue 97% subscription; **international 37%**; NA 95% US.
- Seasonality: Q4 biggest bookings, Q1 highest FCF.
- One Big Beautiful Bill Act (Jul 2025 tax law): immaterial to tax rate. CA deferred-tax valuation-allowance release helped the provision (noisy tax rate).
