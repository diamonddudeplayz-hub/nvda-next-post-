# NOW Q3 2026 (Oct 28) + the cRPO bridge — the whole debate

The stock fell **27% in a day** on this print. The trigger was a soft **Q4 cRPO guide**,
not the actual quarter (which beat). The question that decides the stock: is that cRPO
softness **accounting/timing** or **real demand deceleration**? Answer: **mostly the former,
with a real-but-smaller underlying deceleration underneath.**

## Q3 actuals — a beat across the board
- Subscription revenue **$3.988B, +20.5% cc**, +50bps above the high end of guide.
- **cRPO $13.65B, +21% cc — a ~100bps BEAT** vs guidance (but a *smaller* beat than the ~200bps beats of prior quarters).
- RPO $29.7B (+22.5% cc). Renewal rate **98%**.
- Non-GAAP operating margin **32.6%, +150bps above guide**.
- FCF margin 20% (Q3 is seasonally the low FCF quarter); Q3 FCF $822M (below cons ~$923M).
- **AI net-new ACV +65% YoY**; AI ACV target **raised again, $1.5B → $1.7B+** for 2026 (3rd raise from the original $1B). AI $1M+ deals +4.5x; agentic AI consumption in production +13x YTD; EmployeeWorks hit $100M ACV in <3 quarters.
- 680 customers >$5M ACV (+23%).

## Q4 guide — the scare
- Subscription revenue $4.245–4.255B (**+21.5% cc** — fine, roughly in line).
- **cRPO +18.5% cc** — ~120bps **below** Street's ~19.7%, and a decel from Q3's +21%.
- Op margin 32%.
- FY26: subscription revenue guide **raised** to $15.785B (+21% cc); **FCF margin raised to 36%** (from 35%) on lower capex.

## The cRPO bridge — why 21% → 18.5% is mostly NOT demand
Management's decomposition of the Q4 cRPO guide (confirmed/accepted by JPM, TD Cowen, BofA):
1. **Early renewals pulled into Q3.** ~half the Q3 cRPO beat came from renewals that closed early — which *robs Q4 to pay Q3*. Pure timing; the growth is already booked, just in the prior quarter. (This is the "contracts pulled forward" point.)
2. **Higher on-prem / public-sector mix in Q4: ~−25bps.** On-prem (sovereign, Federal) is recognized differently and doesn't build cRPO ratably. A *good* result (strong Fed demand) that optically dents cRPO.
3. **T4C (termination-for-convenience) provisions: ~−50bps.** Federal + other contracts with T4C clauses can't all sit in RPO/cRPO under ASC 606 — ~50bps of subscription backlog growth is **excluded from cRPO but still hits revenue**.
4. **Deployment / professional-services carve-out: ~−50bps.** For select large AI deals, NOW now bundles delivery expertise; ASC 606 carves that value out of subscription into professional services. **It returns to subscription revenue on renewal** (which, mgmt notes, usually happens *earlier* than the ~3-yr contract term). A structural *acceleration* dressed as a near-term headwind.
5. **Armis adds ~+175bps tailwind** to Q4 cRPO. So **organic** cRPO is ~**16.8–17.3%**.

**So the honest read:**
- A large chunk of the 21%→18.5% move is **timing (pull-forward) + accounting (T4C, on-prem mix, PS carve-out)** — i.e., it hits the *leading indicator* but **not revenue**. Subscription revenue (the thing that becomes cash) **beat in Q3 and was raised for the year**. JPM: the cRPO decel "is largely on account of revenue drivers moving out of scope of cRPO to a greater extent on a temporary basis."
- **The caveat (don't skip it):** even after adjusting for all of the above, organic growth is *still* decelerating modestly as the company matures at scale (BofA: "even after adjusting for T4C and deployment services, the 4Q growth targets still fall short of consensus"; JPM models FY27 subscription growth ~18.9%). So it's **not 100% accounting** — there's a real, gentler deceleration underneath the optical one.

## Why the stock *really* fell 27% (it wasn't the quarter)
cRPO was the trigger; the de-rate (50x → 22x NTM P/E over the year) is the **"AI disrupts SaaS / seat compression"** structural fear. A noisy cRPO print on a stock the market is already scared about = violent move. The bull/bear is in `analysis-is-this-the-one.md`.

## The one thing bulls still can't point to
The AI monetization that's *supposed* to offset seat compression (AI ACV +65%, $1.7B target) **has not yet shown up in the leading indicator** — cRPO is decelerating, not inflecting up. Management says the reacceleration lands in 2027 as consumption flywheels ("hockey stick"). That's the load-bearing claim, and it's a promise, not yet a number.
