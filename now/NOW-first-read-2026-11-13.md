Why NOW fell 27% on 29 Oct ($150.13 to $109.67): Q4 cRPO growth guided at 18.5% against 19.5-20% expected; 2027 subscription growth framed near 18%, about 3 points below 2026; a smaller beat than usual; 25bp of subscription revenue moved to professional services in Q3 and 50bp in Q4 as forward-deployed engineers are added (RBC, BofA). JPMorgan argues most of the cRPO gap is scope and accounting. BofA says it does not "fully subscribe to management explanation." AI ACV target raised to $1.7B from $1.5B.

Scenario sketch for NOW, 12 months, probabilities mine: $83 (15%, growth slips to 15%, 16x 2028 EPS of $5.20), $99 (25%, 18x $5.50), $126 (40%, consensus $5.87 at today's 21.4x), $164 (20%, 28x). Weighted about $120, +20%, against +9% for Adobe on the same method. The NOW figure rests on non-GAAP EPS that excludes about $1.77 a share of stock pay.
