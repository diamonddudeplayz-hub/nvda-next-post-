# NOW valuation (Nov 2026) — what's priced in, and the range

Analysis, not advice. Built off the Q3 FY26 10-Q + guide + TIKR. Price **$101.97**,
market cap **~$105.8B**, EV **~$107B** (roughly net-debt-neutral: $7.5B debt vs ~$7.0B
cash+securities + $2.05B illiquid strategic investments).

## FY26 run-rate anchors
- Subscription revenue ~**$15.8B**, total ~**$16.3B** (+21% cc).
- Non-GAAP operating income ~**$5.1B** (~31.5% margin). **GAAP** op income is ~a third of that (SBC + amort).
- Non-GAAP FCF ~**$5.8–5.9B** (36% margin, *adjusted* — adds back M&A cash costs).
- Non-GAAP EPS ~**$4.03**; GAAP EPS ~**$1.15**.

## Current multiples (the de-rate is the story)
| Metric | Now | A year ago |
|---|---|---|
| NTM P/E (non-GAAP) | **22.5x** | ~50x |
| NTM EV/EBITDA | **14.9x** | ~35x |
| NTM MC/FCF | **15.8x** | ~42x |
| NTM FCF yield | **6.3%** | ~2.4% |
| EV / FY26 revenue | ~6.6x | ~13x |

A ~20% grower with 98% retention, asset-light, 36% FCF margin, at a **6.3% forward FCF
yield**. On an absolute basis that is not expensive; vs its own history it is the cheapest
it has ever been.

## What the price is implying (reverse-DCF intuition)
At ~$107B EV on ~$5.9B FY26 FCF, with the FAD **2030 target of $30–32B subscription
revenue + Rule of 60**, the market is paying **~9x 2030 FCF** (if targets are hit). That is
a *structural-decliner* price for a company still guiding ~20% growth. **The price is
discounting the bear case** — that NOW largely **misses** the 2030 plan (gets to the mid-$20Bs,
not $31B) or that AI compresses its multiple to a no-growth level. You are buying near, not
far from, the pessimistic scenario.

Rough, not a model — but the **skew is favorable**: ~−12% to +135%, and the stock already
sits near the bear outcome. That asymmetry *is* the reason it's interesting.

## Consensus view
- **Sell-side: Buy/Overweight, avg target ~$160 (+50%+)**, 28 Buy / 1 Hold / 1 Sell. Frame: cRPO is noise, AI is accretive ("system of action… not disintermediated"), Control Tower is the governance layer AI needs. (KeyBanc Sell $110 / UBS Hold $140 are the skeptics.)
- **The price, however, is the real "consensus"** — and at 22x / −40% YoY it is pricing **AI cannibalization**. So analyst-consensus and price-consensus *disagree*. That gap is the opportunity or the trap.

## The non-consensus view (what I actually think the debate is)
It is **not** "is the cRPO quarter bad" (that's noise) or even "is AI good for software" in
the abstract. The sharp, under-priced question is a **take-rate race**: can NOW convert AI
*workload* into dollars (via consumption "assists") **faster than AI deflates its seats**?
- The 10-Q gives the live scoreboard few are watching: **subscription gross margin falling 80%→74%** = AI is a *cost today* (hyperscaler GPU rent) that lands **before** the assist revenue. So right now you can see the cost of the AI bet in the financials but **not yet the payoff**.
- **Non-consensus call:** the stock is priced as if the take-rate race is already lost (bear multiple), while the *operational* evidence (AI ACV +65%, assists +500% M/M, 50% of net-new non-seat) says the race is live and early. If assists inflect into cRPO **and** GM troughs and recovers, you get a double re-rate (growth reaccelerates *and* multiple expands). That combination is not in the price.

## What else to look at / what could catch a tailwind
- **Subscription GM inflection (74% → back toward 80%)** — the cleanest, most overlooked EPS lever; signals assists are out-earning their compute cost.
- **Q4 cRPO (late Jan)** — Q4 is the big bookings quarter; the real tell on the "2027 reaccel."
- **AI ACV → cRPO conversion** — the moment bookings color hits the leading indicator, the de-rate reverses.
- **Security (Armis/Veza) compounding >25%** and pulling the core — re-rates the whole story (and de-risks the $10B goodwill).
- **Federal reacceleration** ("AI operating system for government") — but watch the **DOJ/Army investigation** (tail risk on the biggest customer).
- **Rate cuts / "SaaS-is-dead" fear fading** — NOW re-rates hardest because it fell hardest.
- **Buyback** — $4.2B left (~4% of the company at $102); cheap stock + strong FCF = optionality.

## Verdict
At 22x / 6.3% FCF yield you're buying a cash-generative ~20% compounder **near its bear
case**, where the AI-monetization engine is visibly *starting* (bookings) but not yet
*proven* (cRPO, gross margin). Downside looks ~−10–15% (already de-rated); upside is +70%
to +130% if the take-rate race is won. The one thing you must believe to own it: **AI is
net-accretive to ServiceNow's dollars, not just its workload.** The 10-Q says that's
genuinely undecided — which is exactly why it's a barbell *long*, not a sure thing.
