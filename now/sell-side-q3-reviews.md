# NOW Q3 — sell-side synthesis (Oct 29, 2026)

Three notes, all reiterate constructive, all frame the quarter as noise. Plus the skeptics.

## JPMorgan — Overweight, PT $150 → **$160** (Samik Chatterjee)
Title: "Noisy Quarter and Outlook with cRPO Accounting; However, Limited Changes in
Underlying Momentum with Potential Upside from Acceleration in AI Monetization."
- Thesis: NOW is "the **system of action** for enterprise workflows… positioning ServiceNow to benefit from the adoption of agentic AI **rather than be disintermediated by it**." Hybrid (seat + consumption) monetization lets AI usage translate to revenue "independent of seat counts."
- On cRPO: the Q4 decel "is **largely on account of revenue drivers moving out of scope of cRPO to a greater extent on a temporary basis**." Investments in professional services are "temporary"; divergence between cRPO and revenue "reversing in time."
- Raised PT *because a higher multiple looks more sustainable* on visibility into out-year growth drivers, before even baking in AI-monetization acceleration.
- Valuation: $160 PT = ~18x EV/uFCF on CY28 uFCF of $8.6B. Models FY26 rev +22.2%, FY27 +18.9%. Premium justified by higher growth (19% vs 15% peers) + better uFCF margin (37% vs 27% peers).

## TD Cowen — Buy, PT $165 → **$160** (Derrick Wood)
Title: "Noisy 3Q & Guide, But AI Monetization North Star Still Holds."
- "Model noise & lower outlook will **revive some AI disruption concerns**, but we remain constructive on MT growth drivers against a lower bar."
- cRPO guide "below… includes 50bps of headwind from T4C contracts in backlog, 25bps from on-prem Fed mix, and <25bps from new PS carve-outs."
- "AI adoption ramp remains steep" (AI NNACV +65%, target to $1.7B). Notes NOW "is known to rebound after a soft qtr," mgmt reiterated FY30 targets ($30–32B subs, 30% from AI).
- **Scenarios:** Base = high-teens cRPO cc FY26; Upside = >20% cRPO + margin expansion; Downside = mid-teens cRPO, margin compression, flat FCF margin.

## BofA — Buy, PO **$180** (Tal Liani)
Title: "Focus on weak CRPO guidance, but underlying trends remain solid."
- "AI shines but 4Q guidance dims the print." cRPO +21% cc beat; op margin +130bps above Street.
- "We **don't fully subscribe to management explanation** for the quarter weakness, yet remain constructive… we see these fluctuations as growing hiccups." ← the fairest line: management's bridge is *mostly* right, but not a complete alibi.
- Softer demand outlook "partially attributed to accounting" (T4C, deployment/PS carve-out); but "even after adjusting… 4Q growth targets still fall short of consensus."
- Valuation: FCF yield 3.8% ('26E) → 5.4% ('28E); P/E 37x ('26E) → 25x ('28E).

## The skeptics (the Sell/Hold that express the real bear case)
- **KeyBanc — Sell, $110** (Jackson Ader). The lone Sell. Prices the structural "AI disrupts seat-based SaaS" view: that the de-rate is *justified*, not an opportunity.
- **UBS — Hold, $140** (Karl Keirstead), downgraded/assigned Hold.

## Net
Street direction ~unanimous bullish (avg target ~$160, +50%+), ratings held through the
crash. The debate isn't the quarter — it's **terminal value**: is NOW the layer AI needs
(JPM's "system of action… rather than disintermediated") or an incumbent AI erodes
(KeyBanc). The cRPO noise just gave both camps a reason to dig in.
