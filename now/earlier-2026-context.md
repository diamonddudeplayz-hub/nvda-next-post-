# NOW — earlier-2026 context (Q1, Q2, FAD, conferences)

Context for the Q3 crash: the de-rate was already most of the way done by mid-year, and
management laid out explicit long-term targets at the May Financial Analyst Day.

## Financial Analyst Day — May 4, 2026 (the targets that anchor the bull case)
- **2030 targets: $30B–$32B subscription revenue** (roughly double the 2026 ~$15.8B), **Rule of 60+**, SBC below 10% of revenue by 2029.
- **AI = 30% of ACV by 2030.** Three other >25%-CAGR vectors: Security & Risk (Armis + Veza), CRM (crossed $1.8B ACV), Data & Analytics (RaptorDB, Workflow Data Fabric).
- The monetization pitch (Gina): a 20-person support team costs ~$1M/yr (90% labor, 2% ServiceNow). Agentic AI resolves 75% → headcount to 5 → customer total cost −65%, but the freed seats convert to **6.5x more AI-agent consumption**, so **total ServiceNow spend grows >5x**. "Fewer seats, far greater value."
- Autonomous-worker consumption: GenAI task = 1–10 assists; agentic = more; **autonomous AI specialists = 15x+ the assists of GenAI.** By year 5 an AI customer spends ~4.5x the initial assist entitlement.
- Margin claim: "AI reasoning is <10% of cost to serve" → inference costs don't wreck gross margin; customers "pay for a resolved outcome," not tokens. $300M annualized AI cost savings into 2026 ($100M '25 + $200M '26).

## The McDermott thesis (repeated at GS Communacopia Sep 8 + Citi Sep 9 + every call)
- NOW = the **"AI Control Tower" / "Super Intelligence Control Tower"** — the governance, visibility and *execution* layer AI needs. "Rules and rails of the enterprise." Moat = **context** (CMDB + context engine, 100B workflows / 7–8T transactions over 22 years).
- **Build-vs-buy:** DIY on LLMs costs "5–10x more" than running on ServiceNow (integration, security, maintenance). "Vibe slop."
- **Action Fabric:** exposes the "system of action" to ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini headlessly — third-party agents call in and **burn assists**, so NOW monetizes *other* front doors. Action Fabric assists +500% M/M in Q3.
- Security push: Armis (cyber asset graph, OT/IoT) + Veza (identity/access graph) + SecOps → "fastest-growing cybersecurity company at scale," ambition to be #1; "could be bigger than all of ServiceNow in a few years."
- Partnerships: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini Enterprise, NVIDIA (kill-switch via NVIDIA Sentry), AWS, Microsoft — all route through the Control Tower.
- Style note: McDermott is *highly* promotional ("one-of-one," "trifecta," Armis = "our Instagram," repeated AI-target raises). Weigh the salesmanship.

## The pattern across 2026
Every quarter: **beat on revenue + margin + AI ACV, raise the AI target**, but cRPO is
*stable-to-decelerating* and the stock de-rates anyway. The AI story keeps showing up in
*bookings color* (ACV, deal counts) but **not yet in the top-line leading indicator**.
That gap — real AI traction vs a decelerating cRPO — is the entire NOW investment debate.
