# ServiceNow — business & operations (from the Q3 FY26 10-Q + calls)

## What it is
ServiceNow sells cloud software that **digitizes and automates workflows** across an
enterprise, on one platform (the "ServiceNow AI Platform") sitting on a single data model
(the CMDB — configuration management database — + "context engine"). It started as the
system of record for **IT service management (ITSM)** and expanded across the enterprise.

Five product areas: **Technology** (ITSM/ITOM/ITAM — the core), **CRM & Industry**, **Core
Business** (HR, finance, procurement — "EmployeeWorks"), **Creator** (app dev), and **Security
& Risk** (SecOps + Armis cyber-asset + Veza identity). The pitch: it's the **governance /
execution ("Control Tower") layer** that enterprise AI needs — the place agents are
inventoried, permissioned, killed, and where work actually gets *done* (deterministic
workflow), as opposed to probabilistic model output.

## How it makes money (the model that matters for the AI debate)
- **97% subscription, 3% professional services.** Multi-year contracts, invoiced annually in advance, recognized ratably. **98% renewal rate.**
- Historically **seat-based**; now shifting to **hybrid** — seats + **consumption ("assists")**. ~**50% of net-new ACV is now non-seat-based.** This is the crux: the bull case is that AI *usage* monetizes through assists **independent of seat counts**, offsetting any seat compression.
- **AI-native packaging** (FY26): tiers Foundation / Advanced / Prime; AI embedded in all tiers; ~20–30% price uplift to adopt AI. AI ACV target raised 3x ($1B → $1.7B+).
- Go-to-market: **76% direct sales, 24% channel/SI.** International 37% of revenue (NA 63%, EMEA 25%, APAC 12%; US = 95% of NA).

## Scale & unit economics (Q3 FY26 / 9mo)
- Subscription revenue **$3.99B Q3 (+21%)**, $11.54B 9mo (+23%). FY26 guide ~$15.8B subs.
- RPO **$29.7B**; ~46% is current (cRPO, next 12 mo). RPO +22% / cRPO +21%.
- **Non-GAAP** op margin ~32%, subscription gross margin ~**74%** (down from 80% — see findings).
- **Non-GAAP FCF 9mo $3.1B (+20%)**; FY26 FCF margin guide **36%** (capex *cut*: 9mo capex $346M vs $630M). Asset-light.
- Customers >$5M ACV: **680** (vs 553 yr ago). 
- Headcount: being **cut via "AI-driven workforce optimization"** (−1,602 in Q3; $167M severance 9mo).

## Operational character (the things to hold in mind)
1. **Incumbent moat = context + integration + switching cost.** 100B+ workflows, ~7–8T transactions, 20+ yrs of CMDB data. Management claims DIY-on-LLMs costs "5–10x more." This is the whole "AI needs us vs AI replaces us" question.
2. **The model is consciously migrating from seats to consumption** to stay on the right side of AI. Unproven in the top line yet (see valuation + cRPO bridge).
3. **Acquisition-heavy lately:** ~$9.2B spent in 9 months (Armis, Veza, +5), levering the balance sheet for the first time. Security is now a balance-sheet bet.
4. **Seasonality:** Q4 = biggest bookings (budget flush); Q1 = highest FCF. So a Q3 bookings read is the *weakest* seasonal window — Q4 is the real tell.
5. **Federal is a major, concentrated vertical** — one US federal channel partner is ~11% of revenue / 20% of receivables, and there's an active DOJ/DoD investigation (see findings).
