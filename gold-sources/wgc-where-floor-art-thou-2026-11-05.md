# WGC Gold Market Commentary — "Where floor art thou?"

Source: World Gold Council, Gold Market Commentary. Author: Johan Palmberg. Published 5 November 2026.
URL: https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/gold-market-commentary-october-2026

## October in review

- **Gold finished October at US$3,913/oz, down 6.3% m/m**, declines across all major currencies tracked.
- Drivers: Fed rate-path repricing, speculative de-grossing, temporary rotation from equities and gold into bonds. "Much of the selling may have been derivatives-led."
- COMEX managed money net positions fell US$7bn (53t) in October, after a larger drop in September.
- Global gold ETFs **defied the decline: +US$3bn (19t) of inflows**. Europe led, then Asia; North America small decline.

## The floor framework (two of three conditions)

Spot gold ~25% below its January all-time high. Rather than name a floor, WGC asked: what conditions were historically in place when gold recovered during a downtrend? Bear regimes = 50-day MA below 200-day MA for 20+ trading days. A signal "fires" when a reading is in the most extreme 30% of bear-regime months. Three indicators:

1. **Dislocation** — gold trading well below its 200-day MA (how stretched the sell-off is).
2. **Dollar strength** — a sharp DXY rally vs the past 52 weeks (dollar-driven weakness tends to be temporary).
3. **Positioning washout** — speculative futures positions (managed money + smaller traders) falling to unusually low levels vs open interest.

### Table 2: Indicator performance since 2005

| Condition | N | Median 3m fwd | % positive |
|---|---|---|---|
| All bear months | 24 | 3.2% | 63% |
| At least 2 of 3 | 10 | 4.2% | 90% |
| 3 of 3 | 5 | 9.8% | 100% |

3-of-3 episodes: Oct-08 (+28.2%), May-12 (+8.4%), Nov-15 (+16.3%), Dec-16 (+8.9%), Sep-22 (+9.8%).

**Today: two of three in place.** Gold well below 200-day; dollar rallied into top tier. **Positioning remains elevated — not yet washed out.** WGC: "a further shake-out in futures positioning would need to take place before a more durable recovery can take hold." Framework speaks only to the next three months.

## Technicals + conclusion

- Gold at a downside "extreme" (10% below 200-day), above support from the **38.2% Fibonacci retracement of the entire rise from 2015 at US$3,857/oz**. A test of $3,857 "may yet be seen, although that level would likely become an attractive entry point for investors."
- Setup for recovery "building but not yet complete." Backdrop points to a correction, not a repeat of the post-2011 downtrend.
- **Key quote for the DCA post:** "For strategic investors, averaging into weakness has historically made sense for an asset that has returned close to 9% a year since 1971. So the more useful question is not where the floor is, but whether weakness is becoming an increasingly attractive opportunity to add."
