# US Treasury — Quarterly Refunding & Long-End Buybacks (Aug–Nov 2026)

Primary sources for Leg 2 (fiscal dominance / Treasury buying back its own long end). All quotes verbatim from Treasury's own pages.

## 1. August 5, 2026 — Q3 Refunding Statement (sb0590, Dep. Asst. Sec. Brian Smith)

URL: https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0590

> "Treasury anticipates that, over the course of the upcoming quarter, it will purchase **up to $38 billion in off-the-run securities across buckets for liquidity support** and up to $25 billion in the 1-month to 2-year maturity bucket for cash management purposes."

## 2. August 19, 2026 — Long-end buyback size increase (sb0607)

URL: https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0607
Title: "Treasury Announces Increased Sizes of Nominal Long-End Liquidity Support Buybacks Beginning September 9"

> "The U.S. Department of the Treasury is increasing, by at least double, the size of liquidity support buyback operations for longer-dated nominal coupon securities (the 10-year to 20-year sector and the 20-year to 30-year sector). **The current maximum size of $2 billion per operation will be at least $4 billion per operation.** This change is effective September 9, 2026 and will be in effect for the remainder of this refunding quarter (through November 4, 2026)."

(This is the event Post 2, "The Current Thesis on Gold," cited as the doubled $4bn/operation running through Nov 4.)

## 3. November 4, 2026 — Q4 Refunding Statement (sb0678, Asst. Sec. Hunter McMaster)

URL: https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0678

- "$125 billion of Treasury securities to refund approximately $97 billion of privately-held Treasury notes and bonds maturing on November 15, 2026." New cash from private investors ≈ $28 billion.
- Buybacks:

> "Treasury anticipates that, over the course of the upcoming quarter, it will purchase **up to $70 billion in off-the-run securities across buckets for liquidity support** and up to $25 billion in the 1-month to 2-year maturity bucket for cash management purposes."

→ Quarterly liquidity-support buyback envelope **$38bn (Aug) → $70bn (Nov)**, ~84% higher.

## 4. Tentative Schedule of Treasury Buyback Operations — November 2026 Quarterly Refunding

URL: https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/221/Tentative-Buyback-Schedule.pdf
"For Publication November 4, 2026." Schedule runs Nov 2026 into early Feb 2027.

Per-operation maximums (all "Liquidity Support"):
- **Nominal Coupons 10Y to 20Y: $6 billion** (e.g., operations settling 11/5, 12/3, 12/23, 1/13).
- **Nominal Coupons 20Y to 30Y: $6 billion** (e.g., settling 11/18, 12/9, 1/7).
- Nominal Coupons 7Y to 10Y: $4 billion.
- 1Mo to 2Y: $4bn (liquidity support) / up to $12.5bn (cash management).
- TIPS 1Y–10Y: $750 million; TIPS 10Y–30Y: $500 million.

→ Long-end per-operation cap progression: **$2bn (standard) → $4bn (Sept 9 temporary) → $6bn (standing, Nov refunding).**
