# WGC Gold Demand Trends Q3 2026 — Executive Summary + Supply/Demand Table

Source: World Gold Council, Gold Demand Trends: Q3 2026. Published 3 November 2026.
Authors: Louise Street (Senior Markets Analyst), Krishan Gopaul (Senior Analyst, EMEA).
URL: https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/gold-demand-trends/gold-demand-trends-q3-2026

## Summary

- Total Q3 gold demand (incl. OTC) **1% lower y/y at 1,369t**. Y-t-d demand **3,881t** virtually matches the 2025 record (3,884t); value a record **US$566bn**.
- **Central bank buying jumped to 321t in Q3**, lifting y-t-d to 675t (+6% y/y).
- **Gold ETFs reversed Q2 outflows, adding 211t in Q3** — driven by concerns over fiscal sustainability and inflation.
- **Bar and coin demand 19% lower y/y at 268t** — investors waited for a clearer price trend.
- **Jewellery demand fell 17% y/y to 313t**, weakest Q3 in the data series; value +2% y/y to US$43bn.
- Technology +3% y/y (AI-related manufacturing offset high-price drag).
- **LBMA (PM) gold price averaged US$4,262/oz in Q3** — 3rd strongest quarterly average on record, +23% y/y.
- Mine production +3% y/y to 1,071t (Q3 record). Total supply −1% to 1,369t; recycling −8% y/y to a three-year low of 314t.
- Outlook: central bank buying likely to at least match 2025 after a surprisingly strong Q3.
