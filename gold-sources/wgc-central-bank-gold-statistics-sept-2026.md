# WGC Central Bank Gold Statistics: September 2026

Source: World Gold Council, Goldhub Insights. Author: Marissa Salim (Senior Research Lead, APAC). Published 4 November 2026.
URL: https://www.gold.org/goldhub/gold-focus/2026/11/central-bank-gold-statistics-september-2026

- Central banks reported **61t of net purchases in September**, the highest monthly total of 2026 so far.
- Led by **Poland and China**; also net buyers: Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, Jordan, Czech Republic, Bolivia.
- **Year-to-date reported net purchases: 228t, 7% lower than the same period last year.**

Important distinction (reported vs estimated): this series "comprises solely publicly reported changes. This differs from our Gold Demand Trend statistics, which consist of aggregate reported changes as well as an estimate for unreported buying."

→ This is the honest-caveat source: **reported-only y-t-d buying is 228t (−7% y/y)**, while the GDT total (reported + estimated unreported) is **675t (+6% y/y)**. The gap is the ~57–63% unreported theme.

Note: Azerbaijan (SOFAZ) reports quarterly and is excluded from monthly totals. By-country and y-t-d charts include changes of ≥1 tonne only.
Source: IMF IFS, respective central banks, World Gold Council.
