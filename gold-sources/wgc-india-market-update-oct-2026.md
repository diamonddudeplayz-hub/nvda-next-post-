# WGC India Gold Market Update: Gold Demand Gets Festive Boost

Source: World Gold Council, Goldhub Insights. Author: Kavita Chacko (Research Head, India). Published 17 November 2026.
URL: https://www.gold.org/goldhub/gold-focus/2026/11/india-gold-market-update-gold-demand-gets-festive-boost

- Gold prices under pressure Oct into Nov: ended October −6.3% at **US$3,913/oz** (2nd consecutive monthly decline), driven by expected further Fed rate hikes, a stronger USD, and investor shift to bonds. Fell a further ~1% to **US$3,870/oz by 12 November**. Y-t-d down 11% USD, 4% INR.
- Festive buying (Dhanteras/Diwali, Dhanteras 6 Nov) was healthy and broad-based across jewellery and bars/coins; lower prices supported demand. Retailer estimates: value demand +15–25% y/y, volumes flat to slightly higher. Consumers opting for lighter pieces.
- **Indian gold ETFs: 5th consecutive month of net inflows in October** (INR35bn / US$364mn); holdings +2.2t to 126t; 31.3t added y-t-d (+22% y/y). Momentum carried into November. Investors using dips as buying opportunities.
- Gold futures trading cooled: avg daily volumes −30% m/m to 12.7t (lowest since Jan 2025).
- Imports rebounded to ~70t in October (from 23t in Sept) but Sept+Oct (~93t) well below the 2021–25 average (~180t); unofficial supply and recycled old-gold filling the gap after May import-duty hike.
- Jan–Oct 2026 official imports US$47.36bn (−6% y/y); gross volumes ~470t (−14% y/y).
