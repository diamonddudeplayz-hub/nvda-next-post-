# WGC China Gold Market Update: Robust Demand in October

Source: World Gold Council, Goldhub Insights. Author: Ray Jia (Head of Research, Asia Pacific ex-India). Published 17 November 2026.
URL: https://www.gold.org/goldhub/gold-focus/2026/11/china-gold-market-update-robust-demand-october

- **PBoC reported +26t in October — largest monthly buy in three years (since Oct 2023), 24th consecutive monthly purchase.** Holdings now **2,435t = 8.1% of FX reserves** (down from 8.5% in Sept, reflecting the price drop, not selling; value of gold reserves −5.3% on the month despite the tonnage add).
- LBMA Gold Price PM USD −6.3% in October; SHAUPM (RMB) −5.9%. Y-t-d loss 7.1% USD, 9.4% RMB (RMB strength a factor). **LBMA PM closed at US$3,870/oz on 16 November, the lowest daily close in 2026.**
- Chinese gold ETFs: 4th consecutive monthly inflow, +6.3t in October, total holdings a month-end record 310t (62t added y-t-d). Modest outflows emerged in early November as equity-stabilisation measures diverted interest.
- SGE withdrawals +2% m/m to 94t (unseasonal strength) on dip-buying bullion investment + jeweller restocking; still −24% y/y on jewellery weakness.
- Structural shift: bar & coin investment exceeded jewellery consumption in 2025 for the first time ever, and again y-t-d 2026 (VAT reform late 2025 benefited bullion buying).
- Gold imports rose to 129t in September (+41% y/y).
