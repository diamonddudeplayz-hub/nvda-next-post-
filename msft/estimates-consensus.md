## 20-yr context
Revenue FY07 $51B → FY26 $332B (~10%/yr, accelerating to 18% in FY26). EPS FY07 $1.49 → FY26 $17.28
(~13%/yr, accelerating). The Nadella-era re-rating: from a 10x "value" stock (2013) to a ~25x quality
compounder. Today's 24x FY27 is near the high end of its modern range — the opposite of GOOGL's
below-trough 17.8x.

## Three lines that matter
1. **Quality + FCF-positive** — the safest financial profile of the three (47% EBIT margin, 27% ROIC,
   never FCF-negative), but priced for it (15x EV/EBITDA, −13% off high).
2. **Azure +48% accelerating, clean** (no hardware gross-up), $677B RPO — demand is backlog-floored.
3. **The risk is valuation + OpenAI concentration** (~11% Azure / ~45% RPO) + partner dependence (least
   vertically integrated on silicon/models). Not a demand problem — a price-and-concentration problem.
