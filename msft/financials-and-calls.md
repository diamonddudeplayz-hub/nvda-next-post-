# MSFT — financials & earnings-call notes (Q2 FY26 → Q1 FY27)

Microsoft fiscal year ends June. Primary source for financials: Q1 FY27 10-Q (period ended Sept 30 2026,
filed Oct 28 2026) + the call. Figures from Amy Hood's remarks + TIKR.

## Q1 FY27 (quarter ended Sept 30, 2026) — the latest
- Revenue **$92.3B (+19%)**; operating income +21%, **operating margin ~50%** (49.7%); **EPS $4.89**
  (+18% adj. for OpenAI). Gross margin 68%.
- **Microsoft Cloud $63.9B (+30%**, accelerating); **Azure +48%** (ahead of expectations), guided
  **+49–50% cc** for Q2. Foundry revenue **>3x YoY**.
- **RPO (backlog) $677B, +73%**; weighted avg duration 2.3 yrs; ~30% recognized in next 12 months.
- M365 Commercial Cloud +18%; Copilot DAU +50% QoQ / +5x YoY; customers with >50k Copilot seats +8x YoY
  (Volkswagen 330k). GitHub: agents open **>1/3 of all PRs**.
- **Capex $45.2B** (incl. finance leases $7.4B; cash PP&E $39.4B). ~3/4 short-lived (CPU/GPU). **CFO $58B
  (+29%); FCF $18.6B** (positive). Returned $10.2B to shareholders.
- **OpenAI ~11% of revenue** in the quarter (Gabriela Borges Q); OpenAI is the largest single RPO
  component (~45% at Q2 FY26). First Vera Rubin GPUs docked; Maia 200 live (1.7x tokens/watt).
- Capex guide: Q2 >$50B; **calendar-2026 ~$170B** (lowered from ~$190B on 3P delays + a useful-life
  change shifting finance→operating leases); H2 FY27 accelerating. **"Remain FCF-positive in FY27."**

## FY26 full year (ended June 2026)
Revenue **$331.8B (+18%)**; Microsoft Cloud >$214B (+27%); **Azure >$100B run-rate, +41%** full year;
operating income >$155B (+21%). 88 data centers added; capacity ~doubling in 2 years. Anthropic stake
gain $3.2B ran through OI&E.

## The 5-call through-line
- **Azure accelerating every quarter:** +37% (Q3 FY26) → +39% (Q4) → +48% (Q1 FY27), guided +49–50%.
  Clean services growth, capacity-constrained (demand > supply all year).
- **Copilot = "the new OS for work":** per-seat → per-seat **plus consumption** (the GitHub model extended
  to all of work), expanding TAM + ARPU. E7 suite (Copilot+E5+Entra+Agent 365) landing millions of seats.
  Foundry the app/agent platform (120k customers); Agent 365 the governance control plane (50M+ agents).
- **Own stack, but partner-leaning:** MAI first-party models (MAI-Base-2, etc.) "comparable within 3–6
  months at a fraction of the cost"; Maia silicon (own chips) — but both are *behind* Gemini/TPU and
  Trainium in scale/frontier. MSFT explicitly runs a **heterogeneous fleet** (NVIDIA + AMD + Maia) and a
  **multi-model** strategy (OpenAI + Anthropic + MAI + open) — "harness decoupled from the model."
- **The ROIC defense (Satya/Amy):** fungible one-pool fleet; allocate capacity by LTV across Azure +
  Copilot + GitHub + security + R&D, not to maximize the Azure KPI; capex mostly short-lived (CPU/GPU)
  and "already contracted for most of its useful life"; software extends asset life. "Never been more
  confident in long-term returns."
- **OpenAI relationship:** reworked post-recap — MSFT keeps frontier-model **IP royalty-free to 2032**,
  a rev-share through 2030, and its equity; OpenAI is a large Azure customer. Win-win framing, but the
  **concentration (11% Azure / ~45% RPO) is the single biggest MSFT-specific risk.**

## Balance sheet / returns
Net debt ~$69B (0.33x EBITDA — small). LTM gross margin 67.7%, **EBIT margin 47.1%, ROE 34%, ROIC 27%**
— the highest-quality financial profile of the three hyperscalers. Dividend yield 0.8% (pays + buys back,
unlike GOOGL which halted buybacks and AMZN which pays nothing).
