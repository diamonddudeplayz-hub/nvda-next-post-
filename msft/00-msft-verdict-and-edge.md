# MSFT at $485 — verdict and edge (Nov 22, 2026)

Built off 5 calls (Q2 FY26 → Q1 FY27 + the Mar MS conference) + the Ignite 2026 keynote (KEY01.docx),
9 TIKR estimate files, and 3 Nov sell-side notes. **Microsoft's fiscal year ends June**, so "Q1 FY27"
= quarter ended Sept 30, 2026. Price **$484.52 (Nov 20 close)**, −13% off the $559 high. Analysis, not
advice. Three-way comparison: `../HYPERSCALER-SCORECARD.md`.

## Verdict in five lines
1. **The highest-quality, safest-balance-sheet, lowest-beta name in the complex** — 47% EBIT margin,
   27% ROIC, **β 1.04** (vs GOOGL 1.23, AMZN 1.50), and the only one of the three that stays **free-cash-
   flow positive throughout the buildout** (FY27E FCF +$29B vs GOOGL/AMZN negative). If "ballast" is the
   word, this is the most literal expression of it.
2. **Azure is accelerating and clean** — +48% (Q1 FY27), guided **+49–50%** for Q2, ~300bps above Street.
   Microsoft Cloud $63.9B (+30%). No TPU-hardware gross-up optics (unlike Google) — it's services demand.
   RPO (backlog) **$677B, +73%**.
3. **Copilot is the enterprise-AI distribution leader** — the unique edge. 450M M365 seats, millions of
   paid Copilot seats (DAU +5x YoY), GitHub Copilot 50M users (1/3 of all PRs agent-generated), Foundry
   120k customers (revenue tripling). "Copilot = the new OS for work." Neither GOOGL nor AMZN has the
   Office/Windows/GitHub enterprise lock-in.
4. **But it's the priciest and least derated** — **15.0x FY27 EV/EBITDA** (vs GOOGL 10.3x, AMZN 9.9x),
   24.2x FY27 P/E, and only **−13% off its high.** You pay full freight for the quality; the margin of
   safety is the thinnest of the three.
5. **The idiosyncratic risk is OpenAI concentration + partner dependence** — OpenAI is **~11% of Azure
   and ~45% of RPO**, and MSFT is the **least vertically integrated** (Maia silicon is newest/smallest
   scale vs TPU/Trainium; MAI models are emerging vs frontier Gemini). So MSFT leans hardest on NVIDIA +
   OpenAI. The OpenAI IP (royalty-free to 2032) is an asset, but the dependence is a real concentration.

## Where the edge is (the bull case)
- **Copilot as the enterprise OS** — the one thing no one else has. The M365 install base is the widest
  AI distribution channel in enterprise, now monetizing via **per-seat PLUS consumption** (the GitHub
  playbook extended to all of work) — a genuine TAM + ARPU expansion. If Copilot consumption inflects
  like GitHub did, there's revenue upside not fully modeled.
- **Azure clean acceleration** on a fungible fleet, with efficiency gains the company converts to revenue
  in-quarter. Demand > supply; the constraint is capacity, not demand.
- **FCF-positive quality** — in a VIX-36 "show me the cash" tape, the one hyperscaler that never dips
  negative on FCF is a defensive edge. Lowest beta, highest returns on capital.
- **Street unanimously bullish** — BofA Buy $630, targets ~$636 avg (+31%), conviction raised post-Ignite.

## Where the price might be right (the bear)
1. **Valuation + least derated.** 15x EV/EBITDA and −13% off the high is the thinnest cushion of the
   three. A multiple that full needs the growth to keep delivering — any Azure wobble (capacity timing,
   token-pricing variability — both flagged) and the stock has room to fall.
2. **OpenAI concentration (~11% Azure, ~45% RPO).** This is the circular/lab-funding risk *concentrated*.
   If OpenAI stumbles or renegotiates, MSFT has the most direct exposure of the three. The recent OpenAI
   recapitalization already changed how MSFT books the equity stake (gains/losses now flow through OI&E).
3. **Least vertically integrated** — most dependent on NVIDIA (GPUs) and OpenAI (frontier models). Maia/
   MAI are real but behind TPU/Trainium/Gemini. So MSFT captures less of the "own-silicon un-scarce the
   shovel" upside than GOOGL/AMZN.
4. **Consumer/Windows drag** — Windows OEM −22%, PC market weak, Xbox soft. A real (if small) anchor.
5. **FCF is positive but thin** — NTM MC/FCF ~133x; capex (~$195B FY27E implied) has compressed FCF even
   though it stays positive. The "FCF-positive" edge is directional, not a fat yield.

## What you need (the actionable read)
- **MSFT is the best *business quality* and the safest *balance sheet* of the three — but the worst
  *entry* and the most *concentrated idiosyncratic risk* (OpenAI).** It's the "sleep-at-night but no
  bargain" name.
- **For your slot (value + derate + low idiosyncratic risk), it ranks third** — GOOGL is cheaper and more
  derated; AMZN has the better cloud at a similar multiple with its own silicon. MSFT's quality is real
  but it's priced for it (−13%, 15x EV/EBITDA) and the OpenAI concentration is a genuine idiosyncratic
  risk, not a clean one.
- **Own it only on a real pullback** (toward the low-$400s / ~20x FY27, ~12x EV/EBITDA) where the quality-
  at-a-discount actually shows up. At $485 it's the hold/quality-ballast leg, not the value-add.
- **Watch:** Azure growth (does +48% hold?), Copilot consumption inflection, the OpenAI relationship/
  concentration, and capex-to-FCF conversion.

**Bottom line: Microsoft is the highest-quality, lowest-beta, FCF-positive hyperscaler with the best
enterprise-AI distribution (Copilot) — genuinely the safest *business*. But it's the most expensive, the
least derated, the least vertically integrated, and the most OpenAI-concentrated. For a value-conscious,
scaled-in entry it's third of the three; it's the name you buy on a flush, not at the high.**
