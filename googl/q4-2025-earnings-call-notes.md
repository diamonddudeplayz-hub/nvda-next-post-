# Alphabet Q4 2025 earnings call — Feb 4, 2026

The quarter that set up 2026: Gemini 3 launch, first $400B revenue year, Cloud re-acceleration,
and the first $175–185B capex guide. Speakers: Pichai, Schindler, Ashkenazi. (Completes the
4-quarter transcript set: Q4'25 → Q1'26 → Q2'26 → Q3'26.)

## Headline numbers
- **FY2025 revenue >$400B** ($403B, +15%) — first time.
- Q4 consolidated revenue **$113.8B, +18%** (17% cc) — accelerated, led by Search + Cloud.
- Operating income $35.9B, **op margin 31.6%** — dinged by a **$2.1B Waymo SBC charge** (Waymo's $16B raise re-valued the equity; mostly in R&D).
- OI&E +$3.2B (nonmarketable equity gains). Net income +30% to $34.5B; **diluted EPS $2.82** (+31%).
- **Record OCF $52.4B** (Q4), $164.7B FY. **FCF $24.6B** (Q4), **$73.3B FY** — the last "clean" FCF year before the 2026 negative-FCF era.
- Cash + securities $126.8B; LT debt only $46.5B (pre the 2026 debt binge).
- Q4 buyback $5.5B + dividend $2.5B (still returning cash — contrast with Q3'26 where buybacks went to zero).

## Segments
- **Google Services +14% to $95.9B**; Search & Other **+17% to $63.1B** (retail-led), op margin 41.9%.
  - YouTube ads +9% to $11.4B (lapping 2024 election spend).
- **Google Cloud +48% to $17.7B**, op income $5.3B (**margin 30.1%** vs 17.5% yr ago). Backlog **$240B, +55% QoQ**.
  - GenAI-model revenue **+~400% YoY**. 14 product lines >$1B. Gemini used by 120,000+ enterprises.
- Other Bets rev $370M, op loss $3.6B (incl. the $2.1B Waymo charge). Waymo: **20M+ autonomous trips, 400k+ rides/wk**, $16B raise.

## Gemini 3 — the inflection
- Gemini 3 Pro: "fastest adoption of any model in our history," 3x the daily tokens of 2.5 Pro.
- Antigravity (agentic dev platform) 1.5M WAU in ~2 months.
- 1P model APIs **>10B tokens/min** (up from 7B prior quarter). Gemini app **750M MAU** (+100M QoQ).
- **Apple deal announced**: Google is Apple's **preferred cloud provider** and will help build the
  next-gen **Apple Foundation Models on Gemini**. (This later becomes "Apple pretraining on TPU.")

## Search + AI monetization (the debate, early form)
- Search saw "more usage than ever." Daily AI Mode queries per user **doubled** since launch; AI Mode queries **3x longer**; ~1 in 6 AI Mode queries non-text.
- AI Overviews **>2.5B users/month**; AI Mode **>1B MAU**.
- Still "early stages of experimenting with AI Mode monetization" — testing ads below AI responses,
  Direct Offers. **This is the key setup:** a year later (Q3'26) the Search decel is blamed partly
  on AI Mode monetizing below traditional search — i.e. the lever flagged here as "not yet pulled"
  is still being pulled.

## Capex / outlook (the first big guide)
- **FY2026 capex $175–185B** (later raised twice — to $180–190B in Q1, then $195–205B by Q2).
- Depreciation: $15.3B (2024) → $21.1B (2025), guided to "meaningfully increase" in 2026 — the
  P&L drag that shows up all year.
- "Supply-constrained" framing begins here and runs through every 2026 call.

## Why it matters
Q4'25 is the "before" picture: clean $73B FCF, buybacks intact, debt $46B, Cloud just
re-accelerating to +48%. By Q3'26 every one of those flipped — FCF negative, buybacks zero,
debt $125B, Cloud +103%. The business got better *and* far more capital-intensive. The Apple/Gemini
deal here is the seed of the TPU-merchant-silicon story that becomes central to the bull case.
