# GOOGL at ~$283: is it the play? (Nov 6, 2026)

Written off the Q3 2026 call + the TIKR/TipRanks snapshot. This is analysis, not advice.

## The setup in one paragraph
Alphabet is ~30% off its high ($408 → $283), trading at **20x NTM P/E on ~20% forward
EPS growth and ~25% revenue growth**, with net cash, 44% ROE, and the best custom-silicon
franchise outside NVIDIA. The de-rate is real (29x in Dec → 20x now) and almost entirely
about one fear: **AI is eating Search.** That's the whole debate.

## Darren's thesis, tested against the data
> "Google dropped, if it's better-than-expected there's upside, and Google benefits in the buyers-win world."

Partly right, partly needs correcting:

**Right:** the drop is a Search-fear overshoot, and Search Q3 was *not* a demand miss.
Search still grew +12% to $63.3B with **query growth at a 5-year high**. The deceleration
from +17% (Q2) was mostly (1) FX — "the single largest driver," (2) a World Cup pull-forward
comp + an APAC de-minimis change, and (3) **AI Mode monetizing below traditional search today**.
Point 3 is the key one: it's an *un-pulled lever*, not lost demand. Management is only now
scaling ads into AI Overviews + AI Mode, taking it international "for the first time," adding
multiturn ads. So "better than expected" has a clear mechanism: monetization catches up to
usage. That's the bull case, and it's credible.

**Needs correcting:** Google is **not** a clean "buyers win because capex discipline is
forced on them" play. That post-election logic (hyperscalers rally because they're *forced*
to stop spending, so FCF recovers) does **not** fit Alphabet:
- Q3 capex **$51.4B**, FY26 guide **$195–205B**, 2027 **"increase significantly."**
- **FCF went negative: −$9.1B in Q3; LTM FCF $19.7B, down from $53B two quarters ago.**
- **$924B** of future purchase commitments + $152B leases.

Google is spending *into* the buildout as hard as anyone. So if your reason to own it is
"capex gets reined in and cash comes back," you've got the wrong name — that's more an
Apple/Meta-discipline story. **Google's bull case is growth-capex that pays off through
Cloud + TPU + Gemini, with Search as the risk, not capex restraint.**

## Where Google actually is the strongest Mag 7 name for *our* thesis
The "own-silicon wins" leg is not a theory here — Q3 made it concrete:
- **Cloud +103% to $30.7B**, op margin 36.2% (from 23.7%), backlog **$561B**.
- TPU external customers now include **Apple (pretraining its foundation models on TPU)** and
  **Anthropic**, plus Citadel, Jump, Oracle, Crux AI. ~100 TPU customers. TorchTPU (with Meta)
  lowers the switching cost onto TPU.
- Kurian: payback on AI servers **<2 years, own silicon ~half that**; majority of infra
  contracts are **5-year committed**.

This is the part that doubles as a short-NVDA hedge: every credible TPU/Trainium deployment
is a unit of demand that doesn't go to a merchant GPU at NVIDIA's margin. Google is the
cleanest listed expression of "the thing that un-scarces the shovel." Apple on TPU is a real
tell.

## The bear case (be fair to it)
1. **Search disruption is real, not imaginary** — AI assistants (incl. Google's own AI Mode)
   monetize worse than 10 blue links, and third-party AI search/agents divert queries. The
   +17%→+12% decel is the first number that lets bears say "it's showing up." If AI Mode
   monetization *doesn't* close the gap, the de-rate is justified, not an opportunity.
2. **FCF is negative and commitments are enormous.** At 20x P/E it's "cheap"; at a −1.1% NTM
   FCF yield it isn't generating owner cash right now. If AI ROI disappoints, $924B of
   commitments is the overhang.
3. **OI&E noise** — net income swings on the SpaceX mark (+$98B Q2, −$8.2B Q3). Watch
   operating income (+30%, clean), not headline EPS.

## What makes it "better than expected" (the things to watch)
- **AI Mode / AI Overviews monetization** closing the gap to traditional search — now scaling, intl, multiturn. This is the single most important variable.
- **Search growth re-accelerating** once the FX + World Cup comp + 2025-accel lap rolls off (H1 2027).
- **Cloud + TPU revenue ramp into 2027** (TPU system revenue is mostly a 2027 event per mgmt).
- **FCF inflection** — capex is front-loaded; the question is whether revenue/margin catch the cash back up in 2027–28.

## My read
At 20x on 20% EPS growth with net cash, a 36%-margin hyperscaler growing 100%, and a
merchant-silicon business Apple and Anthropic are buying, **the risk/reward is more
favorable than the tape suggests — but the entire call is Search.** If you believe AI
Mode monetizes toward parity (and the "incremental query + similar CTR" comments say it's
trending that way), the drop is an entry and the TPU optionality is free. If you believe AI
structurally lowers search ad monetization, no amount of Cloud growth offsets the biggest
profit pool, and 20x is a value trap.

For *our* barbell, GOOGL is the most coherent long against the NVDA/shovel short: same AI
demand, opposite exposure to the scarcity premium (Google *benefits* if merchant-GPU scarcity
breaks, because it sells the substitute). It is **not** the name to own if the thesis you're
expressing is "capex gets cut" — Google is doing the opposite.
