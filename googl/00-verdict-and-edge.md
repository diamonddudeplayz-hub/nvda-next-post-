## The verdict in five lines
1. **GOOGL is the right name for the slot you're hunting** — "cleanest AI-ROI beneficiary, least
   idiosyncratic risk." It captures AI ROI whether value accrues to chips (TPU), models (Gemini),
   cloud (GCP), or apps (Search/YouTube), is **financed by a highly profitable, diversified operating
   franchise (Google Services op margin ~41%) + a fortress balance sheet** [CORRECTION per 2nd-opinion
   review — see `03-…`: it is **NOT** "self-funding" in the FCF sense today; 9mo'26 FCF was −$4.8B and
   it raised ~$147B and halted buybacks to fund the buildout], carries no circular-financing tax, and
   *is* the company taking NVDA's share. That's a better risk-adjusted expression than Micron (memory
   cycle) or NVDA (OpenAI + circular financing).
2. **But correct one thing in the handover:** GOOGL is **not** a "capex gets cut, FCF comes back"
   play. The 10-Q is unambiguous — buybacks went to **zero**, debt +$79B, equity +$43B, preferred
   +$18B, **>$1T of forward commitments**, FCF negative for ~3 years. It's a *growth-capex-that-
   compounds* play. Own it as **ballast/core**, not as a torque trade, and not for cash return.
3. **The entire de-rate is one fear — "AI is eating Search" — and the hard data says it isn't.**
   BofA's October alt-data: Google global search share **91.0%, +100bps y/y**; ChatGPT only **~7%
   of Google in the US and flat/declining m/m**. The debate has already shifted from *demand* to
   *monetization*. That shift is the edge.
4. **The edge = the gap between a price discounting Search disruption and a business where Search
   demand is holding and AI-Mode monetization is a lever not yet pulled.** You're paying **17.8x
   FY27 EPS / ~10x FY27 EV/EBITDA** — cheapest of the quality hyperscalers — for 30% operating-
   income growth, net cash, and free TPU optionality.
5. **The one thing that can break it is also the only thing that matters: Search monetization.**
   Not query volume (that's fine) — whether AI Mode/agentic search monetizes toward parity with
   10 blue links. If it does, this is a 20–45% upside name. If it structurally doesn't, no amount
   of Cloud growth offsets the biggest profit pool and 18x is a value trap.

## Where the price is underestimating (the bull edge, point by point)

**A. Search share is GAINING, not losing — the fear is mispriced.**
The stock went from 29x (Dec'25) to ~18x on the premise that AI assistants divert Search. The
alt-data contradicts it: 91% global share (+100bps y/y), 2.9bn daily visits (+4% y/y), ChatGPT
small and flat. Query growth is at a **5-year high** in Google's own numbers. The market is
pricing demand loss that is not in the data.

**B. AI-Mode monetization is an un-pulled lever, not lost demand.**
The Q3 Search decel (+17%→+12%) was mostly **FX** (mgmt: "single largest driver"), a World Cup
comp, and an APAC de-minimis change — plus **AI Mode monetizing below traditional search *today***.
That last part is the point: management only *started* scaling ads into AI Overviews + AI Mode in
Q3, took monetization **international for the first time**, and added multiturn ads. CTR on
sponsored vs organic in AI Mode is **"already similar."** As the comps/FX roll off (H1'27) and
monetization scales, Search can re-accelerate. That's a mechanical bull path, not hope.

**C. The clean multiple is genuinely cheap, and the GAAP optics hide it both ways.**
FY26 GAAP EPS of $19.59 is junk (inflated by a +$127.5B equity-mark gain). The clean forward is
**FY27 $14.88 → 17.8x at $265**. On EBITDA (n=25, reliable): **10.3x FY27, 7.9x FY28** on low-30s%
growth. Cheapest quality hyperscaler (vs MSFT ~23x). Net cash ~$115B. 44% ROE.

**D. TPU / own-silicon optionality is nearly free.**
Apple is pretraining its foundation models on TPU; Anthropic is a direct customer; ~100 TPU
customers; distribution via Oracle, Crux (Blackstone JV), Broadcom. Payback <2 yrs (own silicon
~half). This is a merchant-silicon business the market barely capitalizes — and it doubles as the
structural short against NVDA scarcity: every TPU deployed is demand that doesn't pay NVDA's margin.

**E. YouTube + subscriptions are the quiet, non-AI cash engine the market ignores.**
>$60B/yr, 150M+ Music/Premium subs growing faster than ads, #1 in US streaming watch-time. It's a
second profit pool that has nothing to do with the Search debate.

**F. The FCF trough is capex *timing*, not broken economics.**
OCF is *accelerating* ($165B'25 → ~$277B'27E). FCF is negative purely because capex front-loads
(~$217B→$337B→$396B). Consensus itself models the inflection to **+$64B (29) → +$141B (30)**. The
market is applying a trough sentiment to a trough-capex year.

## Where the price might be RIGHT (be honest about the bear)

**1. Search monetization may NOT close to parity — the only thesis-killer.**
If AI Mode / agentic answers structurally monetize below 10 blue links, and third-party agents
slowly divert high-intent commercial queries, the largest profit pool ($63B/qtr Search) de-rates
*and* de-grows. The +17%→+12% print is the first number that lets bears say "it's showing up."
This is binary and it is the whole call. Everything else is secondary.

**2. >$1T of forward commitments against 3 years of negative FCF.**
$924B purchase commitments ($213B due within 12 months) + $130B leases not-yet-commenced + energy
take-or-pay out to 2031. If AI ROI disappoints, that's a mountain of committed spend with no owner
cash to cushion it and buybacks already switched off. The balance sheet can carry it (net cash,
$1T assets) — but the *option to be wrong* is expensive.

**3. Cloud's headline growth is partly low-margin TPU gross-up.**
Consensus Cloud revenue $59B→$323B by '28 (5.5x) is flattered by booking TPU *hardware* sales as
gross revenue. Judge Cloud on **operating margin (36.2%, rising)** and segment EBITDA, not the top
line. Segment-EBITDA consensus is single-analyst (n=1) — thin.

**4. DOJ/antitrust remedy overhang.** Known and digested, but the default-search-payment remedy
(and ad-tech case) could dent Services. Manageable, not zero.

**5. OI&E noise / equity marks** make GAAP EPS swing ±$100B (SpaceX, Anthropic). Cuts both ways;
watch operating income, not headline net income.
