# Alphabet Q3 2026 earnings call — Oct 28, 2026

The important one. It's what the stock reacted to (Nov 5 close $282.57, ~30% off highs).
Speakers: Pichai, Schindler, Kurian (Cloud, first call appearance), Ashkenazi (CFO).

## Headline numbers
- **Revenue $127.6B, +25%** YoY (reported & cc). ~$0.5T annual run rate.
- **Operating income $40.6B, +30%**, op margin **31.8%**.
- **OI&E −$8.2B** — unrealized loss on the SpaceX stake (SpaceX fell in Q3). Net income and EPS **fell** YoY purely on OI&E, not operations.
- OCF $42.3B (Q3), $179.6B LTM. Dinged by TPU inventory build + a $5.2B EC-fine cash payment.
- **Capex $51.4B** in the quarter. ~65% servers/networking (6-yr life), ~35% data centers (7–40-yr).
- **Free cash flow NEGATIVE −$9.1B** in Q3; LTM FCF only **$19.7B** (was $53.3B LTM a quarter earlier).
- Cash + securities $265.1B (incl. $90.4B marketable equities). LT debt $125.1B.
- **Future purchase commitments $924B** + $152B pre-commencement leases/other. "Maximum contractual" — many have variable pricing/volume flexibility, so actual spend could be lower.
- Dividend $0.22/sh.

## Segments
### Google Services +11% to $96.3B (op income +17% to $39.2B, margin 40.7%)
- **Search +12% to $63.3B** — retail largest contributor, then finance. **Decelerated from +17% in Q2.**
- YouTube ads +9% to $11.2B (brand-led, living room strong).
- Network ads $7.5B, +1%.
- Subscriptions/platforms/devices +11% to $14.3B (YouTube Music/Premium, Google One AI plans).

### Google Cloud +103% to $30.7B (op income $11.1B, margin 36.2% vs 23.7% yr ago)
- GCP the vast majority; **TPU system sales a "meaningful contributor."** Ex-TPU growth still "incredibly strong."
- **Cloud backlog $561B**; expect to recognize just over 55% over next 24 months.
- 3rd-party bridging capacity came online only at very end of Q3 (limited margin hit this quarter; more in Q4).

### Other Bets rev $372M, op loss $2.0B. Alphabet-level op loss $7.7B (shared AI R&D).

## The Search deceleration — the crux (Philipp's explanation)
Drivers of Q2→Q3 Search decel:
1. **FX — the single largest driver.**
2. Vertical dynamics: APAC retail hit by EU killing the de minimis exemption; media/entertainment normalized after an unseasonably strong World Cup Q2.
3. **Lower monetization of AI Mode queries** — AI Mode queries grew strongly but monetize below traditional search *today*.
- Offsetting bull signals: query growth the **highest in 5 years**; AI Mode queries average 3x longer, often upper-funnel/new commercial intent; "the majority of AI Mode queries are incremental." CTR on sponsored vs organic product listings in AI Mode "already similar."
- **Now scaling ads in AI Overviews + AI Mode** to more users/queries, taking monetization **international for the first time**, plus multiturn ads in the US. => monetization is a lever not yet pulled, not demand lost (bull read).

## TPU — the "own-silicon wins" story in the flesh
- 1P model APIs ~**32B tokens/min**, +40% QoQ, 4x YoY. Gemini app at **1B users**. Gemini 4 Argon + Carbon launched (Carbon #1 on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 59).
- TPU 8t / 8i: up to 2.7x better training / 80% better inference price-perf vs prior gen.
- **External TPU customers named**: Anthropic (direct + via Broadcom), **Apple scaling pretraining of its foundation models on TPU**, Mirendil, Physical Intelligence, Citadel Securities, Jump Trading, Caltech, Cassava, Cirrascale.
- New partnerships to deliver TPU systems with **Crux AI and Oracle** (plus Blackstone JV). ~**100 TPU customers**.
- TorchTPU built with Meta (PyTorch on TPU) — lowers switching cost onto TPU.
- Ross Sandler framed "~$35B of compute per GW of TPU systems"; mgmt wouldn't break out revenue recognized or margins. TPU system sales = backlog first → inventory build (cash out) → revenue on delivery/billing (cash in). Different cash cycle than traditional capex.

## Outlook
- Q4: larger FX headwind (Search/YouTube); Search also lapping the 2025 acceleration (a couple points of drag).
- Cloud: TPU system sales scale in Q4, meaningful revenue ramp in 2027; near-term margin headwind from 3rd-party bridging capacity.
- **FY26 capex $195–205B unchanged. 2027 capex to "increase significantly"** — detail on Q4 call.

## My one-line takeaway
Operations fine-to-great (Cloud +103%, TPU inflecting), but the print carried three things
the market dislikes: Search decel (the fear), negative FCF, and a $924B commitment number —
i.e., Alphabet is spending *into* the buildout, not stepping back from it.
