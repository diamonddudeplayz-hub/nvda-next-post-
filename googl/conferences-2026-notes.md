Color and strategy detail that doesn't show up in the earnings prints.

## Jun 3, 2026 — Special call (capital raise)
Pichai + Ashkenazi. Context for the equity + debt raise.
- **Capex scale**: 2022 ~$31B → 2026 ~6x that (~$180–190B at the time), ~double 2025. 2027 to significantly increase.
- Funding "balanced": strong OCF + debt issuance + equity offering. Debt ~$16B a year ago → ~$100B across 6 currencies. Goal: keep one of the strongest balance sheets in the world while accelerating investment.
- **Tokens**: 9.7T/month two years ago → **3.2 quadrillion/month** (300x+) across surfaces. Model APIs ~19B tokens/min.
- Q1 recap: rev +22% to ~$110B; Cloud +63%, backlog >$460B. Waymo valued **$126B** (Feb), 500k+ rides/week, 11 US cities.
- Framing: demand "meaningfully exceeding available supply" — supply-constrained is the recurring theme all year.

## Sep 8, 2026 — Goldman Sachs Communacopia (Thomas Kurian, CEO Google Cloud)
The clearest articulation of the **full-stack / TPU** advantage.
- Cloud ~**$100B annual revenue run rate**. Winning new customers ~2x as fast as a year ago; 17+ product lines each >$1B.
- **Only provider with first-party solutions across the whole stack**: own silicon (TPU), own Arm CPU (Axion), own frontier models (Gemini), data, security (Wiz), apps.
- TPU: 12th year building accelerators. **2.7x price-perf training / 80% inference / 30% CPU** vs peers. "Accelerator business more than twice the next largest hyperscaler."
- **Payback on AI servers <2 years; on own silicon about half that.** Majority of infrastructure total contract value is **long-term committed 5-year contracts**.
- Three TPU business models: (1) cloud subscription, (2) **capital purchase** (customer buys systems for their own DC — no Google capex for power/space, strong FCF), (3) **neocloud** (Blackstone JV).
- Diversified monetization: "some buy us for silicon, some for models, some for data platforms, some for security" — captures revenue whichever way AI monetizes.
- Cyber (Wiz + Gemini): 90% of Fortune 100 use Google cyber; CodeMender auto-remediates. Thesis: you can only defend AI threats with security platform + AI model combined.

## May 14, 2026 — MoffettNathanson (Neal Mohan, CEO YouTube)
- YouTube total revenue **>$60B in 2025** (ads + subs). The flywheel: creators → 2B daily viewers → monetization.
- **125M+ YouTube Music/Premium subscribers**; subs growing faster than ads (~high teens). First Premium price increase in 3 years (just before the conference).
- **Living room >50% of US watch time** (200M hrs/day). #1 streaming by watch time 3 years running; #1 podcast platform.
- AI across the flywheel: creation tools (Veo/Gemini), recommendations, ad creative. "Ask" features (75M users on viewer-side Ask in April).
- Paid out >$100B to the creator economy over 4 years; 3M creators in the Partner Program.
- **Shorts monetization reached parity with in-stream** in the US and several countries.
- On AI slop / AI music: handled via long-term-satisfaction recommendation signal + likeness detection (Content ID for the AI era); frames AI as augmenting human creativity, not replacing.

### Why these matter to the thesis
The conferences are where the **own-silicon moat** is spelled out: <2-yr (and half-that)
payback, 5-year committed contracts, a merchant-silicon business larger than any rival's,
and now Apple + Anthropic as TPU customers. That's the structural reason Google is the
"un-scarce the NVDA shovel" name, not just a buyer. YouTube is the quieter, non-AI cash
engine ($60B+, subs compounding) that the market ignores while fixated on Search vs AI.
