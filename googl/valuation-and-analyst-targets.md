## The FCF problem (this is the catch)
Free cash flow has collapsed and gone negative as capex explodes + TPU inventory builds.
- NTM levered FCF: $69.8B (Dec 25) → $25.2B (Mar) → $14.3B (Jun) → **−$33.9B (Sep)** → **−$37.3B (Nov)**.
- LTM levered FCF: $46.8B (Sep 25) → $56.6B → $44.2B → $30.8B → $28.7B → **$5.5B (Nov)**.
So on P/E it looks cheap; on cash it is currently *consuming* cash. The two views
disagree, and the gap is capex. (Q3 capex $51.4B; FY26 guide $195–205B; 2027 "significant increase.")
