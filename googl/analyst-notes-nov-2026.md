# GOOGL — sell-side synthesis (Oct 29 Q3 reaction → Nov 12–18 post-selloff)

Two waves of notes in the repo: (1) the **Oct 29** Q3-reaction notes ("Search concerns
intensify" — the target cuts) and (2) the **Nov 12–18** post-selloff updates (alt-data +
agentic). Together they frame exactly the one debate that matters: **is AI eating Search, or
just repricing how it monetizes?**

## The Oct 29 wave — "Search concerns intensify" (target cuts, no downgrades)
Covered in `valuation-and-analyst-targets.md`. Net: the Street trimmed PTs on the Search decel
(+17%→+12%) but **stayed Buy** — BMO 480→425, Citi 447→375, Jefferies 445→400, Cantor 420→350,
Wells 417→371, Piper 400→345, MoffettNathanson 370→345. **Bernstein the lone Hold (~$300–325).**

## The Nov 12–18 wave — the alt-data rebuts the Search-death fear
### BofA (Nov 13) — BUY, PO $380 — **the single most important note in the set**
Similarweb / Sensor Tower / Statcounter data for October:
- **Google global search share 91.0%, +100bps y/y** (US 85.5%, +35bps y/y). **Share is GAINING.**
- Google web visits **+4% y/y to 2.9bn/day, +1% m/m** — **stable/growing, not bleeding.**
- **ChatGPT −2% y/y to 195mn/day, −1% m/m**; in the US ChatGPT web visits are only **~7% of Google's.**
- Gemini web +123% y/y; Gemini mobile DAUs **158mn (+244% y/y, +5% m/m)**; Google mobile DAUs 2.3bn.
- Verdict line: **"Search debate shifts from query growth to monetization."** Exactly the thesis:
  the demand/usage fear is not in the data; the open question is the AI-Mode monetization gap.

### Scotiabank (Nov 12) — Sector Outperform, PT $375 (~24x 2027E P/E)
"AI Winner or Loser? We Still Think Google Is Built to Win." Full-stack + consumer-AI distribution.

### Wolfe (Nov 12) — constructive; three live items
- Google testing a new fast/efficient model **"Hydrogen"** (post-Gemini-4 efficiency push → lower
  cost/latency for agentic/high-volume = a moat). Modest positive.
- **Anthropic IPO multiple-times oversubscribed** (seeking up to ~$90B, biggest IPO ever) — read as
  a positive for **AMZN, GOOGL, META** (their lab bets/compute customers are fundable).
- **OpenAI Q3: revenue $10B but operating loss −$10.9B (−109% margin)**, +200mn non-paying users.
  The "live knife" is still burning cash — relevant to the complex, not to GOOGL directly (Google
  doesn't need OpenAI; runs its own models).

### Morgan Stanley (Nov 18) — Gemini "Tasks" (consumer agent)
Gemini's agentic "Tasks" sets the '27 consumer-agent battle line vs Meta's Muse. **4x model
efficiency** lets Google deploy agents across its billion-user base; starts with app/business
connections that must scale. Read: Google leveraging distribution + efficiency into the agent era.

### Wedbush (Nov 16) — data-center policy risk "more manageable than feared"
Cato Institute call: post-election political/permitting risk to US data-center buildouts looks
**overdone**. De-risks a macro overhang for all hyperscalers/power names (relevant to the $130B
not-yet-commenced energy/DC leases in the 10-Q).

## What the Street is effectively saying
- **Consensus PT ~$375–400** (TipRanks avg $398–400; Scotia $375; BofA $380) vs **$265 spot →
  ~+42–51% implied upside**, Strong Buy consensus, Bernstein the cautious outlier.
- **Nobody credible argues Search demand is collapsing** — the alt-data says Google is *gaining*
  share and ChatGPT is small and flat. The bear case has narrowed to **monetization** (AI Mode
  gap) and **capex/FCF** (the 3-year cash desert), which is exactly where the edge/risk doc lands.
