# Alphabet Q2 2026 earnings call — Jul 22, 2026

Speakers: Pichai, Schindler, Ashkenazi.

## Headline
- **Revenue $119.8B, +24%** (23% cc). Op income **$40.8B, +30%**, margin **34%**.
- **OI&E +$98B** — large unrealized *gains* on equity securities (SpaceX up in Q2; reverses in Q3). Net income/EPS up big on OI&E.
- OCF $39.1B (Q2), $185.7B LTM. **Capex $44.9B.** FCF **−$5.9B** (Q2), $53.3B LTM.
- Cash $242.5B; LT debt $98.2B (up from ~$16B a year earlier).

## Segments
- Google Services +15% to $94.5B; op income +20% to $39.5B, margin **41.8%**.
  - **Search +17% to $63.3B** (retail + finance). YouTube ads +13% to $11.1B. Network −1%. Subs +15% to $12.9B.
- **Cloud +82% to $24.8B**; op income $8.8B (3x+), margin **35.6%** (vs 20.7%).
  - **Backlog $514B** (+$50B QoQ); >50% over 24 months.
  - **First delivered TPU systems into customer data centers in Q2**; began recognizing TPU system revenue.
- Other Bets rev $382M, op loss $1.8B. Alphabet-level op loss $5.8B.

## AI / models
- Model APIs ~**22B tokens/min** (from 16B prior quarter). 9M+ monthly developers.
- Launched Gemini 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, 3.5 Flash Cyber. **Gemini 4 pretraining started** ("most ambitious yet").
- Gemini app ~950M MAU (DAU tripled YoY). Gemma open models 900M+ downloads.

## Capital / guidance
- **Capex guide RAISED to $195–205B** (from $180–190B) — "acceleration in delivery of capacity."
- Capital structure: fund via OCF first, then debt + the recent equity raise; debt portfolio ~$16B → ~$100B in a year. Not planning more equity (ex the ATM for SBC tax).
- 2027 capex to "significantly increase."

## Thesis-relevant
- This is the **peak Search quarter (+17%)** that Q3 (+12%) is compared against — part of the Q3 "decel" is this tough comp + World Cup pull-forward + FX swing.
- Cloud margin inflection (20.7% → 35.6% over the year) is the profitability proof for the "buyers win on their own cloud" leg.
