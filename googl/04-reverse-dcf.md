## Margin of safety, stated plainly
**At $265 you pay for ≈0% perpetual growth, and/or FY27 EPS ~19% light, and/or a permanent de-rate to
17.8x** — three pessimistic assumptions for a 20%-op-income compounder with net cash. **You don't need
the bull case; you need Google not to *shrink*.**
- **Search merely holds** → the ~0% terminal bar is too low → re-rate to **$300–410** (WACC/capex-dependent). **+13% to +54%.**
- **AI optionality shows up** (Cloud/TPU compound, capex rolls over as the new file models) → you bought
  a double-digit grower at a no-growth price → multi-year double.
- **You lose** only if FY27 earnings come in ~15–20% light *and* the multiple stays ≤17.8x — i.e. Search
  structurally breaks, the one trackable risk. Downside ≈ **$210–230**.

## The three levers that move the answer
1. **Capex trajectory — the biggest one.** The new file says capex *peaks 2028 then falls*; if instead it
   stays elevated (the conservative path), fair value drops ~$80/share. **The Feb-3 2027 capex guide is
   the first hard read** — a guide that confirms a 2028 peak is a major bullish catalyst for exactly this
   reason.
2. **Discount rate.** 9% → +54%, 11% → +9%. In a VIX-36 tape the market uses a high discount; as risk
   premia normalize, fair value re-rates up mechanically. You're partly buying a high-discount-rate moment.
3. **Terminal growth.** Every +0.5% ≈ +$20–25/share at 10% WACC.

## Bottom line for tomorrow's decision
**On the actual LSEG capex data, 17.8x is not just "optically cheap" — the DCF says fair value is
~$300–340 (base) with the price embedding ≈0% perpetual growth.** The skew is clearly favorable: **~+13%
to +54% if the business merely holds-to-grows, vs ~−15% to a $210–230 floor if Search genuinely breaks.**
That's a ~2.5:1 reward/risk with the downside cushioned by net cash + the non-Search engines. **Scale in
here, keep powder for a flush toward $230–240 (where it'd be pricing outright multi-year decline), and
let the Feb-3 capex guide + H1'27 Search prints confirm the capex-peak and the monetization.** The one
number that decides it remains Search commercial-query monetization — everything else the price is
giving you at a discount.
