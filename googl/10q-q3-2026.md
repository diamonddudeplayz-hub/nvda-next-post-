**The OI&E story in one line:** 9-month OI&E of **+$127.5B** (vs +$26.6B a year ago) is almost
entirely **unrealized marks on equity stakes** (Anthropic, SpaceX, etc.). In Q3 alone it
*reversed* to **−$8.2B** (SpaceX marked down). This is why GAAP net income/EPS is lumpy and
useless for valuation — operating income (+30%, clean) is the real earnings engine.
Cash-flow statement confirms it: "Loss (gain) on debt and equity securities, net" of
**−$126,755** is backed out of operating cash flow (i.e. it's non-cash).

**The tell:** Alphabet flipped from *returning* ~$44B (buybacks + dividends) to *raising*
~$147B (equity $42.9B + preferred $19.1B + net debt ~$76B) in nine months — **and stopped
buybacks cold ($39.8B → $0)**. OCF only grew +13% because working capital (inventory −$11B,
AR −$10.8B, other assets −$17.8B) absorbed cash for the TPU ramp. Core cash generation is
stronger than the OCF line shows; reported FCF is negative because capex doubled YoY.

## Commitments & backlog — the overhang, quantified
- **Revenue backlog $567.7B**, of which **$560.8B is Google Cloud**. Expect >55% recognized as
  revenue over next 24 months. Includes **$16.8B** tied to the **Crux AI** JV (related party;
  Blackstone venture — Blackstone commits up to $5.0B capital, Google up to $10.0B infra/energy
  backstops). _(Call said $561B; 10-Q says $567.7B — same number, filing is the precise one.)_
- **$924.4B material purchase commitments**, of which **$802.1B** is multi-year technical
  infrastructure + inventory + **energy take-or-pay** + content licenses, "significant majority
  fulfilled through 2031." **$213.4B is due within 12 months.**
- **$130.5B future lease payments on leases not yet commenced** (data centers + energy),
  commencing 2026–2031, non-cancelable terms up to **26 years**.
- Includes a short-term lease (the Q4 "bridging capacity") with ~$6.0B remaining non-cancelable.

**So the forward spend commitment is ~$924B purchases + ~$130B not-yet-started leases ≈ >$1T
contractual**, against which FCF is negative for ~3 years. That is the bear case stated in the
company's own filing. The mitigant management stresses: "maximum contractual" — many have
variable pricing/volume flexibility, so actual spend can be lower, and backlog ($568B, mostly
Cloud) is the demand side that funds it.

## Why this doc matters to the thesis
The call told you operations are great (Cloud +103%, op margin up). The 10-Q tells you the
**financing reality underneath**: total assets past $1T, PP&E +$126B in nine months, debt +$79B,
preferred +$18B, equity issued +$43B, **buybacks to zero**, and **>$1T of forward commitments**.
Alphabet is not a "capex gets cut, cash comes back" story right now — it is spending and
*financing* into the buildout as hard as any name in the complex. The bull case requires the
Cloud/TPU backlog ($568B) and Services profit pool to convert that spend into the 2029 FCF
inflection consensus already models. (Primary source: EDGAR, Alphabet 10-Q, period 2026-09-30.)
