# GOOGL — the decision (capstone, Nov 22, 2026)

The dated record of the call. Price at decision: **$265.29 (Nov 20 close)**, ~17.8x FY27 clean EPS /
~10.3x FY27 EV/EBITDA. Action taken: **small starter position.** This is Darren's thesis, written down.

## The thesis in one paragraph
At ~10x FY27 EV/EBITDA, net cash, the price embeds Google roughly *stalling* (reverse-DCF: ~0–3%
perpetual growth; earnings: a ~19% FY27 EPS haircut vs consensus). But the demand is **proven, not
hoped** — $561B contracted Cloud backlog means the capex question is *capacity, not demand*, so a capex
slowdown reads as supply-catching-up/discipline (good), not a bust. Search — the one real risk — is being
actively monetized (AI-Mode ads now scaling internationally, multiturn, agentic-commerce take-rates) and
its margin is **defended from the cost side** by a fast-falling, TPU-driven cost-to-serve plus *improved*
advertiser ROAS from better intent-matching. TPU/own-silicon is both an emerging business line and the
structural moat (NVDA-hedge + the cost edge that defends Search). You're paying a stalled-business price
for the cheapest, most-derated, net-cash, frontier-model-plus-own-silicon hyperscaler. **Priced for bad,
structured for good.**

## The valuation anchor: 10x EV/EBITDA
EBITDA is the clean metric (strips the equity-mark EPS noise *and* the D&A optics). GOOGL's NTM EV/EBITDA
ran **~14–16x** the past two years (froth ~18x Dec '25); it's now **~10–11x — cheapest in years, ~30%
below its own norm.** Re-rate math (FY27 EBITDA $305.7B, net cash $115B, 12,294M shares):
| EV/EBITDA | Target | vs $265 |
|---|---|---|
| 10.3x (today) | $265 | — |
| 12x | $308 | +16% |
| **13x** | **$333** | **+25%** |
| **14x** | **$357** | **+35%** |
| 15x (its ~1-yr-ago level) | $382 | +44% |
Roll to FY28 EBITDA ($399B): 12x → **~$399 (+50%)**. "Back to its own average and let EBITDA compound"
= **+25–50% over 12–24 months** — brackets the Street ($375–400) and the DCF base ($300–340).

## Entry ladder

- Unlevered. Size so a move to $210 is an "add," not a forced sell. Core/ballast weight, multi-year hold.

## The dashboard (what to watch) + kill-switch
| Watch | Bull confirm | Bear / sell trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **Search revenue growth** (quarterly) | re-accelerates H1'27 as FX/comps roll off + AI-Mode ads scale | decelerates *again* with no monetization offset |
| **Global search share** (Statcounter/Similarweb, monthly) | holds ~91% / gains | **rolls over m/m** → users defecting = thesis breaking |
| **Implied RPM** (Search rev growth vs query growth) | gap closes | gap widens persistently |

| **Cloud operating margin** | holds/expands (not just revenue) | compresses as TPU-hardware mix rises |

**The one number that decides it:** AI-Mode *commercial-query* monetization. The kill-switch is **Search
decelerating again *with global share rolling over*** — that's demand leaving, the one thing cost-defense
and ad-scaling can't fix. Everything else you're getting at a discount.

## Honest caveats (so the record is fair)
- **The residual tail:** AI-answer RPM going into *structural free-fall faster than cost can offset.*
  Unlikely on the leading data (share holding, CTR parity, incremental queries), but unprovable today —
  which is *why* it's cheap and why the position is small.
- **It's partly a rates/duration bet.** Higher-for-longer / more Fed hikes = higher discount rate = these
  names de-rate regardless of the business. GOOGL (net cash) is the most rate-defensive of the three, but
  not immune. Scale slowly; it's a multi-year hold, not a quick re-rate.
- **Crowded-but-cheap.** The thesis is consensus-popular (Street Strong Buy, r/ValueInvesting darling);
  the *price* is not (52-wk low). Holder base is **overwhelmingly passive** (index funds; Norway's SWF
  the biggest non-index holder) — so less hedge-fund-unwind risk, but the edge is entry + holding power,
  not originality.
- **EPS is the noisy metric** (GAAP, equity marks, D&A optics). Value on EBITDA/FCF; EPS is for the
  reverse-DCF sanity check only.

## The call
**GOOGL as the core long** of the hyperscaler basket (AMZN the business-momentum add-on-dips; MSFT the
quality-on-a-flush; small NVDA torque stub). Starter at $265, add into weakness and on confirmation,
hold multi-year, cut only if global share rolls over. Priced for a stall; demand proven; Search margin
cost-defended; ~10x EV/EBITDA on a 30%-EBITDA-grower with net cash. **The uncertainty is the reason
you're paid — sized small to honest, not blind, conviction, with the proof points landing in ~10 weeks.**
