# Alphabet Q1 2026 earnings call — Apr 29, 2026

Speakers: Pichai, Schindler, Ashkenazi.

## Headline
- **Revenue $109.9B, +22%** (19% cc). Op income **$39.7B, +30%**, margin **36.1%**.
- **OI&E +$37.7B** — unrealized gains on non-marketable equities. Net income **+81% to $62.6B**, EPS **+82% to $5.11** (OI&E-driven).
- OCF $45.8B (Q1), $174.4B LTM. **Capex $35.7B.** **FCF +$10.1B** (Q1), $64.4B LTM — note FCF still clearly positive here; it erodes through the year.
- Cash $126.8B; LT debt $77.5B. Dividend **+5%**.

## Segments
- Google Services +16% to $89.6B; op income +24% to $40.6B, margin **45.3%** (highest of the year).
  - **Search +19% to $60.4B** (retail + finance). YouTube ads +11% to $9.9B. Network −4%. Subs +19% to $12.4B.
- **Cloud +63% to $20.0B** (first quarter over $20B); op income $6.6B (3x), margin **32.9%** (vs 17.8%).
  - **Backlog $462B** (nearly doubled QoQ), now includes TPU hardware sales.
  - Revenue from products built on GenAI models grew ~800% YoY.
- Other Bets rev $411M, op loss $2.1B. (Verily deconsolidated; GFiber to combine with Astound.)

## AI / models
- 1P models >**16B tokens/min** (from 10B). TPU 8t/8i introduced at Cloud Next. Wiz acquisition closed March.
- Gemini 3.1 Pro/Flash; Gemma 4. Beginning to deliver TPUs to select customers' own data centers.

## Capital / guidance
- **Capex guide $180–190B** (incl. Intersect). 2027 to "significantly increase."

## The FCF trajectory to watch (across 2026)
| Quarter | Capex | Quarterly FCF | LTM FCF | Search growth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 (Apr) | $35.7B | +$10.1B | $64.4B | +19% |
| Q2 (Jul) | $44.9B | −$5.9B | $53.3B | +17% |
| Q3 (Oct) | $51.4B | −$9.1B | $19.7B | +12% |

Two lines moving the wrong way together through 2026: Search growth decelerating and
FCF collapsing as capex steps up each quarter. That combination is the bear's whole case.
