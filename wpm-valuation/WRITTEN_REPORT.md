## RECOMMENDATION
We initiate on Wheaton Precious Metals with a **BUY** and a **$133 DCF target (+8.5%** vs the $122.60 close). The headline upside is modest on purpose — our base case prices gold at $4,200/oz, *above* today's ~$3,530 spot but *below* the street's $4,500–5,200 decks. The real case is the **asymmetry**: our bull deck ($5,000 gold) implies **$179 (+46%)**, our bear deck ($3,400 gold) implies **$90 (−26%)**, and the position is a levered-but-cost-protected way to own a gold thesis we believe the market is mispricing. We would accumulate on the current gold-driven weakness.

## COMPANY INFORMATION
WPM is the world's largest **precious-metals streaming** company. It pays a miner an upfront deposit for the contractual right to buy a fixed percentage of future production at a low, pre-set price (~20% of spot, or a fixed per-ounce amount). It does not operate mines. That structure is the moat: **no operating costs, no cost inflation, no capex overruns, no single-mine execution risk.** WPM's cost is contractually fixed at ~$600 per gold-equivalent ounce (GEO), so it runs **~75% gross / ~84% EBITDA margins** with ~47 employees. The book spans **45 agreements across 57 mines in 19 countries** (22 producing); ~75% of production comes from **investment-grade counterparties** (Vale, Newmont, Glencore) and ~90% carries parent guarantees. Reserve life exceeds **60 years**, and the balance sheet de-levers from $1.45B net debt to **net cash by 2028**. Revenue is split almost evenly between gold and silver [EXHIBIT: revenue-by-metal donut].

## INDUSTRY OVERVIEW
Royalty-and-streaming companies are the capital-light financiers of the mining industry; the peer set is **Franco-Nevada (FNV), Royal Gold (RGLD), and Triple Flag (TFPM)**. The group's defining feature is that its margins are **structurally immune to the cost inflation crushing producers**: global miner all-in sustaining costs hit a **record $1,930/oz in 2026 (+22% y/y)**, and as gold fell ~7% in the quarter, miner margins compressed ~16% — downside operating leverage. Streamers have none of that. The market pays for the difference: **Barrick trades at ~8.6× earnings and Agnico ~13×, versus WPM at ~24×** [EXHIBIT: streamer-vs-miner multiple bar]. Demand for the sector sits on the gold and silver macro, where a structural central-bank bid is colliding with short-term financial-market volatility.

## INVESTMENT THESIS — *what the market is missing*
**1. Gold's selloff is a financial-layer event, not a break in the structural bid.** Gold is down ~18% YTD and ~37% from its January-2026 high, but the World Gold Council attributes the drop to **rate-path repricing, ETF and speculative de-grossing, and derivatives-led selling** — the *financial layer*. The *structural* buyer has not left: **central-bank demand was 321t in Q3-26 (+42% y/y)**, the PBoC added gold for a 24th straight month, and official-sector Treasury holdings fell ~$164B over the year as reserves rotate into gold. Central banks hold ~8% of reserves in gold versus >70% for the US and Europe — the runway is measured in years, not quarters.

**2. The rate signal has decoupled — so this is a floor thesis, not a price call.** The gold–fed-funds correlation ran **−0.65 from 2004–21 but collapsed to ~0.00 in 2022–26**: the Fed hiked 350bp and gold still *doubled*. The structural buyer overwhelmed the rate signal, which is why we anchor to a **rising floor** rather than forecast a price — and why we hold our base deck conservatively below the street.

**3. Own the streamer, not the metal or the miner.** WPM's fixed ~$600/GEO cost captures gold's upside with a fraction of the downside cost risk that is hammering producers. This is also why **lower gold does not mean lower value for WPM**: its **contracted, fully-funded production grows ~50% to 1.2M GEO by 2030** with no new capital, permitting, or acquisitions required. Rising volume offsets softer price — WPM can earn a *higher* valuation at a *lower* gold price, which is exactly what 2026 has shown (gold −18% YTD while WPM held roughly flat).

**4. Silver is a built-in amplifier.** Silver is ~49% of revenue and moves ~1.6× gold (5-yr beta 1.63, correlation 0.79). It is the reason WPM lagged lower-beta FNV in a down year — and the coiled spring when the cycle turns. **Net: the market correctly discounts WPM's bond-like contracted cash flows at ~5% (as every covering analyst and management do), but it is extrapolating a depressed spot price and under-weighting both the funded volume growth and silver's torque.**

## VALUATION
Our primary tool is a **free-cash-flow-to-equity (FCFE) DCF** with an explicit forecast to 2030 — the end of the funded production ramp — and a terminal value thereafter. Revenue is built bottom-up as (gold oz × gold price) + (silver oz × silver price) + other GEO, with **volume** set by contracted guidance and **metal price as the sensitivity axis** (replacing a generic growth-rate stress). Base deck: **gold $4,200 flat, silver ~$54** (derived from gold via the 1.63 beta). Cost of revenue falls as price rises — the fixed-cost margin-leverage mechanism — so COGS runs ~27% of sales in the base and ~22%/34% in the bull/bear.

On cost of equity, **CAPM gives 10.85% nominal** (Rf 4.95% + β 1.17 × ERP 5.0%), which we show, but we discount the base case at **6% real** for four reasons: (i) the model is in constant dollars, so a real rate is the consistent match; (ii) β 1.17 is almost entirely *gold-price* volatility, which we already model explicitly in the scenario decks — discounting at full β would double-count it; (iii) WPM's residual, price-conditional risk is investment-grade-like (net cash by 2028, no operating or capex exposure); and (iv) **every covering analyst and WPM management discount these streams at 5% — our 6% is already more conservative.** Terminal value blends Gordon growth (g = 1% real) and an 18× EV/EBITDA exit, 50/50.

The base case yields a **$133 target** [EXHIBIT: football-field — bear $90 / base $133 / bull $179 vs $122.60]; the FCFF build independently converges at ~$133, confirming the treatment is internally consistent. **Comparables are a sanity check, not the thesis**: WPM trades at ~15.7× EV/EBITDA versus the ~13.1× peer median — a premium its scale, growth, and margins warrant — so peer multiples on *today's* consensus imply only ~$83–91. The gap to our $133 is **gold normalizing off a compressed spot, not a multiple re-rating** [EXHIBIT: comps table].

## RISKS & SENSITIVITY
The recommendation is, by construction, a view on two variables:
- **Gold price (the thesis itself).** It is our primary sensitivity axis. A sustained return to the $3,400 bear deck takes the target to **$90 (−26%)**. We judge a structural reversal of the central-bank bid unlikely (~5%), but the near-term timing risk is real: gold is at YTD lows and positioning is not yet washed out — we may be *right on structure, not yet on timing*.
- **Discount rate / de-rating.** The entire BUY rests on the market continuing to value these cash flows at a streaming/NAV rate. Applying the full **CAPM 10.85% collapses the target to $88** — our honest downside cross-check, and roughly where the comparables sit.
- **Silver (~49% of revenue)** is more volatile than gold, and the independent industrial-deficit case is contested (BofA models a possible 2026 surplus); we lean on silver's monetary, gold-correlated leg and treat the deficit as optionality.

Diversification cushions the idiosyncratic risks — no single mine dominates and ~75% of volume is investment-grade. If gold keeps falling, the asymmetry (−26% bear vs +46% bull) still favors accumulation, and the recommendation stays BUY across the base and bull decks; only a durable return to a real-rate gold regime, or a market de-rating to CAPM, flips it.

## CONCLUSION
WPM is the **lowest-risk way to express a gold thesis**: contractual, fixed-cost cash flows that resist record mining-cost inflation; **~50% of fully-funded volume growth that earns a higher valuation even at a lower gold price**; and silver torque for the upside. The market is pricing a financial-layer selloff as if it were a structural one, while the central-bank bid that doubled gold through a 350bp hiking cycle remains intact. We rate WPM a **BUY to a $133 base target (+8.5%)**, with an asymmetric path to **$179 (+46%)** in our bull case and limited, quantified downside to $90. **Buy the vehicle, own the thesis.**
