# WPM — Margins & Cash Generation vs. Peers
*Source: LSEG/Refinitiv Company Fundamentals exports (20 Nov 2026), quarterly data aggregated to TTM through Q3-2026. All figures US$M.*

## 1. Profitability vs. peers — WPM is #2, best among streamers

TTM through Q3-2026:

| Company | Model | Revenue | Gross % | Operating % | EBITDA % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Franco-Nevada** | Royalty + energy | 2,378 | **78.4%** | **76.7%** | **90.5%** |
| **Wheaton PM** | Streaming | 3,467 | 74.7% | 72.1% | 84.0% |
| Triple Flag | Streaming + royalty | 502 | 69.2% | 61.5% | 76.4% |
| Royal Gold | Streaming + royalty | 1,760 | 66.2% | 62.7% | 82.5% |

**Read:** WPM is *not* the single highest-margin name — Franco-Nevada edges it. The gap is **structural, not operational**: FNV is a pure **royalty** company (plus high-margin energy royalties) that takes a slice of the top line with ~zero ongoing cost. WPM is a **streamer**, paying the miner a fixed per-ounce price (~$450/oz gold, ~$4/oz silver) on every ounce delivered. That per-unit cost is the entire margin gap.

**Defensible claim:** *"WPM carries the highest margins among dedicated streaming peers (74.7% gross / 84% EBITDA), second only to royalty-pure Franco-Nevada. On a like-for-like streaming basis, WPM leads the group."* Do **not** write "best-in-class margins" unqualified — FNV beats it.

## 2. Margin expansion validates the operating-leverage thesis

WPM margins over time, as the gold price rose from ~$2,000 (2024) to ~$4,000+ (2026):

| Period | Revenue | Gross % | Operating % | EBITDA % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FY2024 | 1,285 | 62.5% | 56.8% | 76.0% |
| FY2025 | 2,315 | 72.2% | 68.3% | 81.5% |
| TTM Q3-2026 | 3,467 | 74.7% | 72.1% | 84.0% |

**+12.2 pts of gross margin in two years.** Because a streamer's cost per GEO is largely **fixed** (~$450 gold / ~$4 silver), a rising realized price flows almost entirely to margin — exactly the price-correlated COGS mechanic in our DCF (COGS% = fixed cost ÷ realized price; base 27%, bull 22%, bear 34%). The filings are the empirical backbone of that assumption: this is *observed* leverage, not a modeling artifact.

## 3. Cash generation — scale is the moat

TTM through Q3-2026:

| Company | OCF | OCF / Revenue |
|---|---|---|
| **Wheaton PM** | **2,807** | **81.0%** |
| Franco-Nevada | 1,859 | 78.2% |
| Triple Flag | 400 | 79.7% |
| Royal Gold | 1,215 | 69.0% |

WPM converts **81 cents of every revenue dollar into operating cash** — top of the group — and does it at the **largest absolute scale** ($2.8B OCF, ~50% more than FNV). Free cash flow was temporarily negative (−$863M TTM) because 2026 was a heavy **growth-investment** year (stream acquisitions deployed the balance-sheet cash that pushed net debt from −$1.15B net cash at FY25 to +$1.45B now). This is growth capex, not operational weakness — OCF is strongly positive and the committed book turns those deals into production/cash over 2027–2030.

## 4. One-paragraph synthesis for the report

> Wheaton generates sector-leading cash at scale — $2.8B TTM operating cash flow at an 81% OCF-to-revenue conversion, the highest of the four streamers — on 74.7% gross and 84% EBITDA margins. Only Franco-Nevada runs higher margins, and only because its pure-royalty-and-energy model carries no per-ounce cost; among dedicated streamers WPM leads. Critically, WPM's gross margin has expanded +12 points in two years (62.5%→74.7%) as gold re-rated, confirming the operating leverage at the heart of our valuation: with cost per gold-equivalent ounce largely fixed, a higher realized price drops almost directly to the bottom line. The 2026 dip in free cash flow reflects a deliberate wave of stream acquisitions — growth spend that lifts the forward production and cash profile rather than a deterioration in the business.
