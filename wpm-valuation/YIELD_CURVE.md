## Read
1. **Not a recession inversion.** 2s10s is positive (+23bp).
2. **Re-flattening toward inversion from the front.** The Fed resumed hiking in 2026 (2y 3.48 -> 4.71), compressing 2s30s from +116bp to +62bp. The short end is climbing to meet the long end. This is the "slow inversion."
3. **The long end is stuck high on fiscal stress.** 30y 5.33%, near the 5.73% Oct-2026 cycle high. The Treasury has scaled quarterly long-end buybacks to up to $70B ($6B/operation, from $38B / $2B), and the long end has not come down: the intervention is not containing it.

## Thesis tie-in
- Corroborates **Leg 2 (near-term headwind):** a rising front end / hawkish Fed is exactly the financial-layer pressure that sells gold short-term, while real yields stay restrictive.
- Corroborates **Leg 3 (fiscal dominance):** an elevated, un-containable long end despite Treasury buybacks is the term-premium / supply signal, the structural backdrop for the central-bank bid. The Fed cannot ease (inflation) and the market will not fund the long end cheaply (deficits + reserve managers diversifying out of Treasuries). Gold holds its structural bid through both.
- One-line for the report (Leg 3): the 30-year sits ~5.3%, near its cycle high, and the Treasury's escalation of buybacks to ~$70B/quarter has not contained it.
