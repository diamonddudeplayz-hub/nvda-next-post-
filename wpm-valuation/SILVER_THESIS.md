# Silver Thesis (expanded) — the second structural leg
_Silver = 49% of WPM 9M-2026 revenue ($1,263M). Not a side-bet; co-equal with gold._

## The setup: the gold thesis with higher torque
Silver's 2025–26 arc mirrors gold's exactly, amplified:
- Early 2025 ~$30 → parabolic **$121.79 blow-off top (Jan 29, 2026)** → **−31% in one day (Jan 30)** → ground down all year to **~$47 (Nov 16, 2026) = 52-wk low, −61% from peak.**
- Historical floor is rising regardless: $17–26 (2022) → ~$47 now. The *financial layer* blew off and was liquidated; the *structural* deficit never changed. Same "two populations, one event" logic as the gold post — silver is the higher-beta expression.

## Structural bull case (consensus; pin exact Moz from Silver Institute World Silver Survey 2026)
1. **Supply is price-inelastic (the core point).** ~70–80% of mined silver is a **by-product** of copper/lead/zinc/gold mines. You can't mine more silver when the price rises — output is set by *base-metal* economics. Mine supply has been ~flat for a decade.
2. **Persistent structural deficit.** The silver market has run a **physical deficit every year since ~2021** (Silver Institute), cumulatively several hundred Moz — funded by drawing down above-ground stocks. _[source exact annual + cumulative deficit Moz]_
3. **Industrial demand >50% of total, and price-inelastic.** Silver is a tiny fraction of each device's cost, so demand doesn't fall when silver rises. Drivers: **solar PV** (largest growth leg, ~20%+ of total demand and rising with global installs even after per-cell thrifting), electronics, EVs (more Ag per vehicle), 5G, AI/data-center connectors. _[source solar Moz]_
4. **Inventory drawdown / physical tightness.** LBMA, COMEX registered and Shanghai stocks drawing down to cover the deficit; **lease-rate spikes and backwardation** in 2025–26 (the Jan-2026 squeeze that drove the $122 print) signal a shrinking free float.
5. **Monetary bid on top.** "Poor man's gold" rides the *same* central-bank / debasement / fiscal-dominance floor as gold — with higher beta both ways.

## The gold:silver ratio = the upside lever we don't pay for
- Spot ratio now **~83:1** ($3,886 / $46.95). WPM strikes guidance GEOs at **60:1** ($4,800/$80). Historical norm ~**65–70:1**; blew to ~125:1 (2020 COVID) and compressed to **~46:1** at the Jan-2026 silver peak.
- Silver **outperforms gold late in precious-metals bull markets** → ratio compression is pure upside. At $3,900 gold: 60:1 → silver **$65**; 50:1 → **$78**. At $4,800 gold (floor): 60:1 → **$80**.
- **WPM impact:** compressing 83:1 → 60:1 on ~28M oz silver ≈ **+$500M revenue ≈ +$370M FCFE** on a ~$3.0B base (~12% uplift) — before any gold move. This is why silver is a *separate* value lever, not just gold beta.

## Why the streamer, not silver itself (ties to the 3-way framing)
Physical silver = lowest risk, pure floor exposure, **no leverage**. A silver miner = max leverage + full cost/execution risk. **WPM = leveraged silver upside at a fixed ~$9/oz cost** (Q3 cash cost $8.83/oz), contracted volumes, no exposure to silver's brutal volatility *in the cost base*. Silver's −31%-in-a-day, −61% drawdown is exactly why you want the fixed-margin vehicle, not the metal or the miner.

## Risk / honesty (for Risks section)
Silver is **more volatile than gold** and **partly industrial** — a recession or a solar-subsidy/thrifting shock hits ~half of demand, and silver amplifies gold's moves in *both* directions. That volatility is the argument *for* the streaming structure, but the downside case (silver back toward $30s with gold) must be shown.
