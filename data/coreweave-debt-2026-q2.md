## Read
- The viral "16%" is the market yield on ~$10B of unsecured senior notes. CoreWeave still pays the ~10% coupon; 16% is only what a new buyer collects.
- Blended cost of debt ≈ 8%, not 16%. The panic overstates it; the bull "3.6% sliver" debunk (= only the $1.25B 2032 tranche) understates the unsecured layer, which is ~$10B.
- No near-term maturity wall: first large maturities 2028+. Stress is about the price/availability of NEW capital, not refinancing something due now.
- JV funding commitment $1.7B (Note). DDTL 4.0 only $2.8B drawn of $8.5B capacity at Jun 30.
